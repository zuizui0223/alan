# Brown2023: concurrent total catches versus temporal guild comparisons

Primary https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2023.106203 (PMC9982679), original XML recovered2026-11-21. Already reviewed in batch7; this is deeper extraction, not an additional primary-read report.

The field experiment used20 pitfall stations,10 with lights and10 interspersed controls, at least15m apart. Five pre-light nights, five lit nights and five post-light nights repeatedly sampled the same stations. Site is a random effect in the reported analysis. Table1 reports concurrent total catches: during-light means18.92(SE1.12) versus10.88(SE.89). The SE denominator and aggregation are not established as uncertainty among independent station means. No station×night data were retrieved; the article requests author contact for data.

Crucially, Methods restrict the subsequent guild and family analyses to experimental stations. Table3 predator means4.96,11.34,3.10 and herbivore means3.28,1.45,2.65 are pre/during/post at illuminated stations, not parallel light/dark contrasts. Table4 Lycosidae means1.26,4.10,.86 likewise describe time periods. Fixed temporal order and missing concurrent guild-control summaries prevent interpreting these ratios as controlled predator-specific ALAN effects. Pitfalls measure capture/activity-density, not attacks, predation rates, moth mortality or plant reproductive outcomes.

`extract_reported.py` preserves36 arm summaries from Tables1,3,4. The `Light` arm label identifies experimental stations even during pre/post darkness. Its seven arithmetic flags are requests for clarification, not substituted values: Table3 herbivore during-minus-post printed−.82 differs from mean subtraction−1.20; Table4 Formicidae6.63 differs from6.36; Table4's five distinct family means sum25.40 during lighting, exceeding Table1's18.92 total under the headings' apparent common denominator. Table3 totals differ by.25 during/post. Different adjusted estimands/denominators or source errors must be distinguished using raw data or author clarification. The check is limited to these tables and comparisons, not a comprehensive correction of the publication.

No numerical repair, SE-to-SD conversion, independent-night n, zero covariance, lnRR adoption, or pooled effect was introduced.
