# Independent computational re-extraction

`validate.py` reads original CSVs directly using Python's standard library; no project extractor/helper is imported. Run from any working directory. This is an independent agent check, not human verification.

All six effect rows agree for means, sample SDs, n, lnRR and first-order delta variance (48 numeric comparisons; maximum absolute difference 3.56e-15). All 36 covariance entries agree (maximum 2.78e-17). Covariance was independently calculated from centred cross-products within each arm, divided by n and the two endpoint means, then summed across arms. Per-unit inputs, detailed comparisons and source SHA256 hashes are saved alongside this note.

Parsing checks: 20 unique pan mesocosms; 60 web records; three exact dates per mesocosm; treatment labels agree across files. Exact equality distinguishes `ALAN` from `no ALAN` (substring matching would fail). Final date is 4 August 2016. Three-date effects average within mesocosm first, preserving n=10 per arm; 30 records per arm are not 30 independent units. Aquatic/terrestrial endpoints use author-supplied origin totals without taxonomic reclassification. Four aquatic totals disagree with sums of the source-designated aquatic family columns: IDs 5 (12 vs14), 7 (172 vs170), 8 (20 vs19), 18 (183 vs182). These discrepancies are retained, not repaired; see summary.json. Body-mass CSV lacks mesocosm IDs and is not used for these six effects.

## Primary-source scope check

[Parkinson et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0240138), cached article.xml, Methods: Experimental design; Mesocosm and ALAN installation; Invertebrate inputs; Spider abundance and body mass and spider prey abundance. Twenty mesocosms received randomized light-by-fish assignments, five per factorial cell. Each illuminated mesocosm had four individual path lights. Thus ten light and ten no-added-light mesocosms support the marginal comparison; four lamps are not four replicates. Fish retention failed and fish terms were omitted by the authors. Source CSVs do not recover fish allocation. Controls experienced skyglow and are not absolute darkness. Pan collection was one overnight sample; web prey were counted on three dates. Counts of web-retained prey are not per-spider attack rates or consumed biomass. Numerical agreement does not validate a broader predation or plant-mechanism claim. Published average spider increase of 51% conflicts with 431/214 (approximately 101% higher); the re-extraction follows raw data.
