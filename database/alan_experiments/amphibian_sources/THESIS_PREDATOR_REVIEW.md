## Design and distinction from chapter 3

Outdoor experiment 1 used 40 mesocosms in ten groups of four. Five groups received one lamp each; five were unlit. Within each group, two tanks received two free-ranging aeshnid dragonfly larvae, two received none (pp.66–67). Thus there are ten tanks per factorial cell but only five independently illuminated groups per light arm. Tanks, individual toads, hormone samples and observation times must not be counted as independent lighting replicates.

Ninety Anaxyrus americanus larvae per tank were added on 27 May 2016, when lamps were switched on. Lamps operated 24 hours daily until metamorphosis ended; reported water-surface illumination is 3.12 lux, SE1.84, range0.03–8.35 (pp.67–68). Consequently this is supplemental illumination including night, not an isolated night-only treatment. The reported LED peak of293nm is not independently verified and is not adopted as a spectral moderator.

Chapter 3 instead describes the 2015 lid×toad×lighting experiment with50 larvae per stocked tank and nighttime lighting (pp.39–41). Chapter 4 is therefore a distinct experiment-year, although the facility/lighting infrastructure may overlap. This is not grounds to count both as geographically independent studies. The recovered Dryad2018 workbook belongs to chapter3; do not relabel it as chapter4 data.

The juvenile phase of chapter4 is laboratory-only, retains no predator treatment and uses offspring from predator-absent mesocosms (pp.69–71). It is not another outdoor predator experiment.

## Reported results and extraction boundary

Survival to metamorphosis was79% without predators and50% with predators, averaged across lighting treatments (p.72). These are predator marginal means, not illuminated/dark cell means. The reported light main effect is t8=-0.52,p=.61 and light×predator interaction t28=.85,p=.40. The interaction did not provide clear evidence of lighting-enhanced predator effects. This is not evidence of equivalence. The endpoint is survival, not observed attacks or a direct per-capita predation rate.

Four treatment means and SE bars are plotted in Fig.1a (pp.82–83), visually inspected, but were not digitized. Neither the survival interaction coefficient nor its SE is reported. A t statistic alone cannot reconstruct an lnRR interaction or its sampling variance. Group-level covariance is also unavailable. No finite effect is added to the effect catalogue or pooled aphid comparison.

The predator main effect is printed as t28=-4.37,p=.002; that pair is inconsistent with an ordinary two-sided t distribution. The printed survival SE values .03/.04 accompanying percentages also leave their scale ambiguous. Preserve these source values; do not silently repair them. Table1,p.80 concerns corticosterone, not survival, and cannot supply the missing survival coefficient.
