The figure caption describes dietary percentage volume, but several plotted categories cannot be a normalized composition as drawn: Rhinolophus has approximately50% beetles plus100% moths within either arm. This approximate reading only flags a scale inconsistency, not digitized effect extraction. Do not renormalize the bars, infer occurrence frequency, or derive a moth lnRR until the main Methods/raw data establish the measure and denominator. The caption marks only P.hesperidus with a within-species light-related moth-consumption increase; the alphabetical labels refer to between-species diets and must not be counted as lighting tests. No absolute prey consumption or attack probability is established by the supplement.

These are not treatment-specific moth mortality denominators. Keep authors' taxonomic labels without assuming all family members have identical hearing or pollination functions.

## Decision

Hold quantitative extraction. Resolve response units, error-bar definition, site/night allocation and repeated sampling, lighting independence, species arm sizes and the exact relationship to the author's2021 conference poster. The poster is a provenance lead, not an independent study. Preserve this possible diet-shift result without elevating it to a generalized increase in bat predation on flower visitors.
