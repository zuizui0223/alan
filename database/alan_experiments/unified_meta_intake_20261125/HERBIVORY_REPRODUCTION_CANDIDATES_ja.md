Within each physical pair average log ratios over dates within species then over species; then equal pair weighting. Sample variance of the three paired summaries /3 and t2 descriptive interval. This is not the previous species-level ratio of arm means or the original author's mixed model. Leaf RR 0.466 [0.114–1.901]; flower RR 0.324 [0.086–1.218]. Terminal sensitivity flower-vs-leaf 0.674 [0.342–1.328]. Damage is sampled retained tissue, not true feeding rate, pollination or seed production. Unresolved source identity/selected support gate preserved; possible source missingness and 3 of 8 paired sites, no extrapolation to full experiment.
