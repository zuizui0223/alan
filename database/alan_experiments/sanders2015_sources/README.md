# Sanders2015: aphid–parasitoid dynamics and temporal reversal

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1038/srep15232 (PMC4607942). Reviewed 2026-11-21 against the cached primary XML, Methods, Results and figure captions. This report was already read in batch3; this extraction does not add a newly reviewed report.

Methods specify nine paired blocks, each with one approximately30-lux cool-white LED cage and one dark cage:18 independent cages. Pots, plants, repeated counts and insect generations are not lighting replicates. The 2014 community contained Vicia faba, three aphid species and three specialist parasitoids. All cages contained parasitoids; there is no parasitoid-presence factorial contrast.

Results report period-average aphid abundance20% lower and parasitoid abundance40% lower under light. Megoura viciae changed from an initially negative response to a positive response late in the experiment (time×light LR8.58,p.0034). These averages cannot substitute for the prespecified final-time endpoint. Aphis fabae showed no clear abundance response (LR2.06,p.1512). Lysiphlebus fabarum nevertheless declined (LR7.12,p.0076), with reported parasitism falling from0.8% to0.4% (z−2.81,p.00501). Other two parasitoid species had no clear parasitism-rate changes. Methods model mummies and host counts, not observed attacks; the exact proportion denominator must be preserved rather than inferred as attack probability. Figure3 lacks the last L.fabarum observation because its host went extinct in most replicates; do not enter a zero rate.

The2014 plant biomass summary excludes two unusually high observations; the excluded unit, treatment allocation and surviving pairs are not specified. The plant-only experiment ran14–28May2015 in the same cages, not simultaneously with the2014 community experiment. Therefore the two plant comparisons do not identify a trophic mediation effect. The paper identifies the first2014 uncertainty as SE; the following ± values are retained literally rather than silently standardized.

`reported_summaries.csv` preserves eight reported summaries with response, time window, source and limitations. They are not eight independent effects. No paired covariance, final-time numerical abundance table, or raw-data/supplement link was found in the inspected XML. Figure1/2 captions give means and SE for nine replicates, but do not supply paired or repeated-time covariance. No figure digitization was performed. No lnRR sampling variance, zero covariance, extra independent study, or pooled effect was introduced.

An independent AI read-only review confirmed cage units, timing, reversal and extraction constraints; this is not human duplicate extraction. Next extraction requires cage×time counts and block identifiers, missing observations, plant exclusions and corresponding plant summaries. Same institutional location alone does not establish overlap with other Penryn experiments; programme independence must be checked before pooling.
