# Jara2025 algal phenols and herbivory: primary Methods and Results reviewed

Reviewed 2026-11-20. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12404802/), DOI [10.1098/rspb.2025.1600](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2025.1600), sections 2(a–d), 3(a–b), Tables 1–2 and figure captions. The indexed primary-page text exposed complete Methods and Results despite direct PMC requests returning browser-challenge HTML.

## Source-derived facts

Chile-collected Mazzaella laminarioides and Tegula atra were studied in a laboratory tidal system. Four-day algal pretreatments compared natural light/dark, continuous darkness and 30-lux nights with 1200-lux days. Phenols used five algae per treatment/tide. Subsequent feeding assays used three snails per tank and two independent 24-hour trials; choice comparisons had nine paired tanks per trial. ALAN-pretreated algae had undetectable phenols and greater consumption. The initial lighting-replicate count is unspecified.

The [Dryad dataset](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.wh70rxwz6) lists DATA.xlsx and README.md. Its variable description instead gives 24/48-hour records and divides consumption by four animals. Paper figure counts and ANOVA residual degrees of freedom also require reconciliation. No raw table was acquired here.

## Extraction implications

This is indoor chemistry/feeding evidence, not an outdoor population or community experiment. Keep it separate from the primary field/mesocosm layer pending scope adjudication. Tank replication in feeding does not establish independent lamps or tidal systems during algal pretreatment. Three snails in one tank are subsamples. Dual-choice responses are paired; no-choice and preference assays are different estimands. Continuous darkness also removes daytime light and is not the appropriate unlit-night control for isolating ALAN.

Undetectable phenols cannot produce a finite conventional lnRR; do not add pseudocounts. Consumption sign, autogenic corrections, per-animal divisor and time labels must be checked against original data before extraction. The printed consumption formula is final-minus-initial corrected by controls, so its sign convention needs checking. Phenols were measured but not experimentally restored or removed independently; the study does not isolate a phenol-mediated causal effect from other altered algal traits. No effect size or precision is added.
