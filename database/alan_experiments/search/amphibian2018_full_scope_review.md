# Final-article phase and interaction audit

2026-11-20. [Published Methods and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6053935/)
sections2b–3b were read through the indexed primary page. Cached `primary.txt`
and `bioc.xml` are access/error responses, not preserved article text.

The outdoor phase confirms one lamp per four-tank group: four illuminated and
five dark groups remained after exclusion. Lids reduced both colonization and
illumination (15.07 versus3.12lux), so their interaction cannot isolate
colonization. Samples on two dates were combined for invertebrate counts.
Relative chlorophyll fluorescence is not a direct biomass measurement.

The subsequent phase used72 selected metamorphs from nine outdoor tanks,
distributed among18 indoor terraria. Its growth and visibility outcomes are
outside the outdoor synthesis and not independent field replication. Daily
feeding does not measure predation.

The published hylid-count light-by-toad interaction reports a negative F
statistic (−3.24; p=.004). Preserve this reporting inconsistency; do not infer
an effect variance from it. The reported colony response is not plant decline
or measured consumption.

Full-paper review is now documented. Previously verified block crosswalks and
conditional group summaries remain separate; final adoption is still pending
endpoint and temporal-aggregation decisions. No new catalogue effect is added.
