# Bolton2017 marine predation: primary Methods, Results and supplement reviewed

Reviewed 2026-11-20. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2016.10.037); public [author-hosted PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5eed14bc3077a438e884d488/t/5f44727cb1389f705f3e53ba/1750422216394/Bolton%2Bet%2Bal_2017_Coastal%2Burban%2Blighting%2Bhas%2Becological%2Bconsequences%2Bfor%2Bmultiple%2Btrophic%2Blevels%2Bunder%2Bthe%2Bsea.pdf).

## Primary-source facts

Ten spotlights illuminated one Sydney Harbour wharf. Day, dark-night and ALAN exposures were scheduled over 21 days. Plates colonized during February–May 2014 were assigned to caged/uncaged conditions. There were three exposure occasions per light condition, each with seven replicate predation units; controls were reused. Each exposure comprised two 11.5-hour uncaging periods. Sonar supplied two filming periods per light condition, divided into 15-minute MaxN observations. It could not measure bites in darkness. GoPro bite observations covered day and ALAN only.

Uncaged cover loss was greater under day/ALAN than dark-night conditions; dark-night abundance was higher despite lower inferred feeding. Responses concern sessile invertebrate cover, fish abundance and bites, not plant reproduction or pollination. Signed cover changes include gains and losses. Supplement S1 is a schematic, not raw measurements.

## Extraction inference

This is one lighting installation with temporal treatment replication, not ten independently treated wharves or independent lighting for every plate. Retain the nested exposure/plate structure and reused controls; raw mapping is needed before assigning a sampling variance. Do not use all 15-minute observations, individual fish or 90 initial plates as illumination n.

A dark-versus-ALAN bite-rate effect is unobserved: inability to count dark bites is not zero predation. Cover loss with caging controls is a different endpoint from observed bites. Signed changes require an additive or controlled before/after effect; a generic lnRR of cover change is unsuitable. Final cover and change-from-baseline are also distinct estimands. A common temporal comparison does not provide independent site replication. Hold quantitative inclusion pending original measurements and exposure-unit mapping; no effects extracted.

Menai2013/Davies2015 used different sites and plates. Potential overlap with other Sydney reports requires a separate audit; shared authors or the word “marine” do not establish duplication.
