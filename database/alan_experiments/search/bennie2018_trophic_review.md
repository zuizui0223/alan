# Bennie2018 trophic experiment

[Primary Methods/Results](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.13240) read on 2026-11-20; accepted PDF cached from Bangor. This is the Penryn54 programme already represented by Bennie2015/Anic2022, not the separate Lizard grassland experiment. Three trophic treatments crossed with dark/amber/white light have six randomized mesocosms per cell. Observations span 2013–2015.

Predator introductions modify the white-light response of slug counts in the authors' longitudinal model; aphid interactions were not significant. Predator abundance was not systematically measured. Slug counts are not observed attack rates. Slugs collapsed across treatments in late2014 and were reintroduced in2015; authors restrict slug analysis through September2014. A blanket last-observation rule would cross this intervention. Flowers are resources, not measured pollination or seed output. Abstract and Results disagree on the direction of leaf C/N response; retain that discrepancy.
