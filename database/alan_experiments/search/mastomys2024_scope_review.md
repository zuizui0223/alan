# Mastomys2024: indoor activity, not field interaction

Reviewed 2026-11-20. DOI10.1016/j.jenvman.2024.123373. [Institutional primary PDF](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstreams/4ab86700-755c-4be9-b24f-b30084202e25/download), Methods2.1–2.3 and Results3 read.

Nineteen wild-caught Mastomys coucha were individually caged. All received the fixed sequence outdoor natural conditions, indoor dark-night baseline, then indoor remote ALAN. The direct-light treatment was added after the remote result, used14 mice, and shortened observation to seven days. No counterbalancing or concurrent dark control separated treatment from time/order. Remote exposure was2lux at cage level but1.5lux inside; direct exposure was2lux at the cage floor. The two ALAN conditions are not strictly equal experienced doses.

Reported activity reductions versus indoor baseline were55.7% and75.5%; these are motion-sensor activity, not population decline or consumption. Outdoor cages excluded direct predator contact. Pollination, seed dispersal and predation are contextual roles, not measured outcomes. Individual identity in a model does not create independent lighting-room replication.

Decision: exclude from the field/mesocosm interaction pool and retain as indoor behavioral context. Do not extract the percentages as weighted effects without covariance. Main text recovered, no pooled effect added.
