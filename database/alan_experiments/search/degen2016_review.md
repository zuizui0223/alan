# Degen2016: spatial trapping is not a light-versus-dark effect

2026-11-20. Final primary Methods and Results read from the [publisher full text](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2656.12540), sections Materials and methods and Results, Table 1. No raw-data extraction or digitization.

Westhavelland, 2012–2013: one 3-by-4 array of 12 HPS streetlights beside a drainage ditch. The analysed contrasts are corner, wing and middle illuminated traps, not illuminated versus dark plots. Lamps, families and repeated years are not independent illumination experiments.

The analysed sample contains 1,312 moths from 13 families. Central-trap capture relative to a spatial null is 0.44; modelled corner/wing attraction is 2.77 and attraction radius 23.3 m. These are not ALAN/dark response ratios. Movement is inferred through a Markov model; pollination, predation and reproduction are not measured.

Decision: exclude from the illumination-versus-dark effect catalogue; retain spatial-mechanism evidence. Flag possible Westhavelland programme overlap before counting related publications as independent. This is distinct from the later radar paper. No new pooled effect or species-level decline estimate follows from this review.
