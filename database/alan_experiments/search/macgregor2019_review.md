# Macgregor2019: pollination success and conditional seed production

[Primary Methods/Results](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecs2.2550) read from the [published PDF](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/149121/1/Macgregor_et_al_2019_Ecosphere.pdf) on2026-11-20. Supplementary targeted web search identified this report outside the indexed search set; it is not a WoS/Scopus hit.

Five field plots received four lighting regimes and darkness, rotating across four4-day runs in2015. Silene latifolia plants had open, daytime-only, nighttime-only or fully caged exposure.169 females included repeated individuals;417 flowers are not independent lighting replicates.

Full-night lighting increased reported seed-setting probability; part-night lighting did not differ clearly from darkness. Seed number/mass were conditional on successful, unparasitized capsules:148 of194 successful flowers;46 damaged capsules were excluded. Larvae moved between capsules in the greenhouse, preventing assignment of seed damage to field treatments. Preserve positive and null findings, not only declines.
