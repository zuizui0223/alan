# Newman2015: chapter-specific review

Reviewed2026-11-20 using [primary thesis](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/79119/3/Rhian%20Newman%20Complete%20Final%20Draft.pdf), printed pp.56–70 and126–136. Direct download returned403; indexed PDF text was readable. No local PDF or checksum is claimed.

Chapter3 manipulates drift at one stream reach over nine nights, three per randomized lighting regime. Downstream nets and within-night samples are subsamples, not independent illuminated reaches. Drift counts are not consumption or predation. Raw night-level counts/covariance remain needed. Printed coordinates have invalid minute values; do not geocode them as coordinates.

Chapter6 uses indoor aquarium feeding-station/refuge detections, not measured prey captures. It is outside the outdoor interaction synthesis. Trials share a lamp and tanks; detection events are not independent replicates. Chapter4 explicitly identifies a published laboratory dispersal report, requiring version matching before any broader synthesis.

This is a selected-chapter review, not complete thesis eligibility. No finite effects added.

## Possible shared drift experiment

[Andersson2015 primary thesis](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:792678/FULLTEXT01.pdf) reports the same brook, nine nights and net illuminances. Distances and lighting times differ. Flag probable shared data, not confirmed duplicates; do not pool as independent studies. Its drift results do not measure fish predation. The publication/grey-literature scope and source discrepancies remain unresolved.
