# Xu2024: activity-density and fauna exclusion

Reviewed 2026-11-20. Full-text/design hold; no finite effect added.

The [final publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S092913932400060X) reports a grassland ALAN-by-fauna-exclusion experiment. Pitfall activity-density and richness increased at night under light; exclusion also had a positive nighttime response. The highlights report no detected interaction. These endpoints do not directly measure consumption or population density. Full exclusion construction, independent lighting allocation, and extractable arm-level variance remain unverified. Direct publisher access returned 403; indexed abstract, introduction and section snippets are not a complete Methods/Results review.

The [2023 preprint](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4385957) has the same six authors and closely matching title, but its abstract reports no significant exclusion effect, unlike the final abstract. Keep this version discrepancy unresolved until full texts and analyses can be compared. Do not substitute preprint estimates or count it as an independent experiment. Its PDF was not recovered. The background source check independently flagged the discrepancy; this is not a second-human numerical extraction.

The [soil-network report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13421007/) is a programme-overlap lead, not an adjudicated duplicate. Its citation of Xu2024 alone does not prove shared units. Verify actual plot IDs and years before pooling.

Decision: retain for full-text retrieval, exclude from current finite-effect pools. No inference that light increased predation or that an undetected interaction establishes equivalence. Completed-review counts must not increase.
