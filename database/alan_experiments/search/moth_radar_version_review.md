# Moth radar: version, sampling and endpoint audit

2026-11-20. [Published paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11494349/),
[preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.10.06.511092),
[author data record](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.kh18932gx).

Published Methods describe95 flights from94 males in one six-lamp array,
June–July2018, Großseelheim. Results instead calls these95 individuals; retain
the discrepancy. The4% attraction result is2/50 illuminated releases, not2/95.
Six lamps are not six independently assigned lighting treatments. The design
uses on/off releases within nights; trial order and temporal blocking require
raw-data verification.

Published Fig.2 has31 hawkmoth and32 lappet flights, filtered by distance and
waypoints. The repeated individual is presented separately. The preprint
caption gives35 and54. Do not transfer those denominators into final-version
effect extraction. Group-specific tortuosity uses different constructions.

This measures movement, not visits, pollen, capture mortality or seed production.
Adult Euthrix potatoria does not feed, so the lappet result cannot be treated
as a measured decline in nocturnal pollination. Light-assisted capture also
selects the experimental sample. Quantitative pooling remains on hold.
