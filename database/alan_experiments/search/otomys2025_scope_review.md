# Otomys2025: indoor activity, outside interaction synthesis

Reviewed 2026-11-20. Primary paper: https://doi.org/10.1080/07420528.2025.2570415
Institutional PDF: https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstreams/84d4bdc4-05e4-48c6-a379-de504880b866/download
Methods pp.1690–1692; Results pp.1692–1695.

Nineteen wild-caught rats experienced a fixed LAB → indoor ALAN (2 lux) → outdoor NAT sequence, with three acclimation and ten measurement days per condition. No concurrent dark room, counterbalanced sequence or independently replicated treatment room is reported. Animals were individually caged; dividers prevented interactions, vegetation was omitted, and the outdoor enclosure excluded predators. The capture coordinate is not a validated outdoor ALAN treatment coordinate.

Motion counts and circadian parameters were measured, not herbivory, prey consumption, pollination or population change. LAB–ALAN differences were not detected for activity (p=.192), amplitude (.640), MESOR (.82) or robustness (.474). These are not equivalence tests. Higher outdoor NAT activity does not isolate lighting: context, temperature, daytime illumination and sequence differ. The reported lmer function and Gamma/log GLMM description require code verification if activity models are reused.

Decision: Methods/Results read; exclude from the field-interaction pool because lighting manipulation was indoor activity-only. This exclusion is independent of the nonsignificant results. Do not assign a wet-habitat experimental effect merely from the species' habitat. No finite effect added; source retained for audit.
