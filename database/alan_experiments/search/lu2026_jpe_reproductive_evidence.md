# Lu2026 JPE: species-specific reproductive evidence, extraction pending

Checked2026-11-20. DOI [10.1093/jpe/rtag160](https://doi.org/10.1093/jpe/rtag160). Publisher abstract: https://www.jpe.ac.cn/EN/10.1093/jpe/rtag160 . This is an abstract-level follow-up, NOT a completed Methods/Results review.

The publisher reports a field light-by-pollinator-access experiment with full exclusion, nocturnal access, diurnal access and open pollination. For self-incompatible Pedicularis kansuensis, the abstract reports reductions of49.1% in Lepidoptera visitation,53.8% in nocturnal pollen receipt, and56.0% in seed set. Self-compatible Euphrasia regelii reportedly showed no clear reproductive response and relied on daytime visitors and autonomous selfing. These are author-reported findings, not independently extracted treatment means or effects. The abstract does not establish the precise treatment contrast underlying each percentage.

## Correction to the screening ledger

The former unqualified note 'no clear seed effect is not zero' omitted the species-specific decrease. Replace it with both findings. Do not convert nonsignificance in Euphrasia to a zero effect, or generalize two species to all self-compatible versus self-incompatible plants. Species identity and breeding system are confounded in this comparison. This source is an important direct reproduction candidate; it is not evidence that the58 wet-affinity decline candidates have the same mechanism.
