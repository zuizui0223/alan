# Westby2020: field-to-chamber diapause experiment

Reviewed2026-11-20; DOI10.1093/jme/tjaa139.

[Author-deposited Methods](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.fj6q573qx) establish two field sites1.5km apart: one illuminated, one dark. Six source demes were represented; two lamps illuminated the single treated site. After cold weather curtailed mating/blood feeding, cages moved into one lit and one dark environmental chamber. All eggs used for experimental diapause assessment were laid after transfer. Thus source demes, cages, egg papers and eggs do not create independent treatment-site/chamber replication. Rotating cages within chambers does not remove treatment/chamber confounding.

The urban wild-egg time series is a separate observational comparison, without an experimental lighting control; rural wild diapause was unavailable because species could not reliably be separated. Do not pool it with the manipulated contrast or infer predation/pollination outcomes.

The [journal endpoint](https://academic.oup.com/jme/article/57/6/1694/5868605) provides an abstract only. Follow-up: author-uploaded journal Methods/Results were read through indexed ResearchGate full text (publication342786030; pp2–4). No local PDF/visual verification. Dryad workbook download returned403. The published light effect does not resolve treatment/site confounding. No finite effect or variance adopted; design fails independent replicated lighting support even if individual-level measurements become available. Retain as mechanistic diapause evidence with explicit scope/design limits, not a field population or interaction effect.
