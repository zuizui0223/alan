## Verified original sources

- Deitsch2023: [original published PDF](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10490269). Four paired forest plots; four repeated72-hour clay-model deployments. Positive arthropod attack proxy; parasitoid trap abundance is not parasitism rate. Data EDI, not Dryad. No adult-moth or plant reproductive endpoint.
- Heinen2023: [original published PDF](https://push-zb.helmholtz-munich.de/deliver.php?id=40269). Seven climate chambers reused in two runs, five chamber-runs per lit schedule versus four dark. Aphid colony growth declined under part-night exposure; plant shoot effects largely uncertain and no ALAN x herbivory biomass interaction. Indoor ecological scope hold, not behavior-only exclusion.
- Parkinson2023: [PMC original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10613537/). Three stations per treatment per habitat, one night. Dark comparisons available despite spectral title. Allometric biomass and capture measure redistribution, not direct consumption.
- Ground invertebrates: [PMC original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10613541/). Eight blocked transects per colour; null diversity with compositional change. Same Dutch long-term programme must be linked.
- Soil isotope allocation: [PMC original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13421007/). Five plots per arm; retain null plant isotope recovery alongside soil effects. Do not reinterpret correlation-network edges as observed interactions.
- Xiao: see existing verified repository screen; greenhouse growth design eligible pending original-data access.
