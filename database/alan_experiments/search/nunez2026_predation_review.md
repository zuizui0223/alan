# Nuñez2026: seasonal apparent predation

Reviewed 2026-11-20; outside-search primary report, published online 2026-10-09 in the 2027 issue. [Publisher](https://ojs.inidep.edu.ar/index.php/mafis/en/article/view/627), [full HTML](https://ojs.inidep.edu.ar/index.php/mafis/en/article/download/627/640/7873?inline=1), DOI10.47193/mafis4012027010103. Methods and Results read from recovered primary HTML.

Table2 primary MCH model: spring OR5.77 (95%CI1.67–19.89), summer OR0.75 (0.34–1.65); light-by-period LRT p=.014. These are tethered-juvenile apparent-predation odds, not natural predation rates. Predator identity and nutritional mediation were not measured. Two periods are not independent studies or replicated seasons.

Design audit: Methods describes three spring blocks/four nights and four summer blocks/five nights, but Table1 reports nine and23 pooled replicates per arm. Summer23 exceeds20 block-night combinations: mapping requires clarification. Main model adjusts night, not block; block-sensitive estimates/uncertainty are not printed although block improved AIC. Missing, injured and uninjured-dead outcome handling remains unresolved. Do not use juvenile totals or pooled observations as independent lighting n.

Table2 secondary spring OR5.27 is a different model incorporating previously published SAO data. Do not mix it with primary estimates or re-extract SAO as new data. Keep model contrasts outside pooled effects pending unit mapping and block-adjusted covariance. Earlier2021 full text remains unread.
