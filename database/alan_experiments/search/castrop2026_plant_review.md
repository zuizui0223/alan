# Plant physiology, herbivory and shared lighting rows

2026-11-20. Primary Methods/Results read in
[Castrop et al. preprint v1](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.02.16.703286).
bioRxiv metadata currently has published=NA. No final publication is asserted.

Two Dutch sites, Radio Kootwijk and Lebret's Hoeve, use the Spoelstra lighting
infrastructure. Each site has one row per red, green, white and dark treatment;
one open-field lamppost per row was used. Plants are nested beneath lamps,
not independent lighting replicates. Site-level paired support is two, not
the10 morphology or8 gas-exchange observations per treatment. Treat overlap
with other Light-on-Nature reports as unresolved programme dependence, not
automatically the same plants or data.

Hypochaeris radicata and Rumex acetosa were regularly watered. Morphology was
reported at29 exposure days; gas measurements occurred at22 and29 days at
different locations. The abstract describes eight weeks of exposure, whereas Methods describes
two months including acclimation and a29-day morphology endpoint. Preserve
this inconsistency; do not label the final endpoint56 days. Site and gas sampling
date differ together. Night flux measurements used intact selected leaves.

Reported contrasts below have no extracted variance. They are not new finite
meta-analysis effects. No detected herbivory difference is not a zero effect.
Transpiration, leaf elongation and photosynthetic rates are not biomass loss,
survival, pollination or measured consumer mediation. Negative nighttime net
assimilation, if present in the raw data, would require an effect metric other
than an unmodified lnRR.

The [cited Dryad dataset](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.cz8w9gjdz) metadata API
returned HTTP200 with an explicit unavailable/permission message for dataset
147192. HTTP success did not provide data. No download or alternative protected
route was attempted. Quantitative extraction is held for access, row-level
aggregation, shared controls and programme-overlap assessment.
