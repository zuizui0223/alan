This is a working metadata route, not full-text recovery. Repository rights are explicitly restrictedAccess. No restricted file access was attempted. Compact metadata evidence is saved beside this note.

Abstract-only screening: field tethering and enclosure assays address juvenile Neohelice granulata survival under general predation and cannibalism, with adult-male abundance also reported. The abstract describes five separated illuminated areas, four consecutive days and new-moon tidal observations. It reports lower juvenile survival under light, but allocation, pairing, handling of missing tethered crabs, arm denominators, time dependence and uncertainty have not been read. Do not label five areas as confirmed five independent replicates per arm. Do not convert abstract percentage reductions into weighted meta-analysis effects. Predator abundance, cannibalism and general-predation survival need separate outcomes; repeated tides would not be independent studies. This is a priority direct-interaction source, not proof of the plant-pollinator mechanism.
