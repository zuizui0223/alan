Methods pp8–14: 90 plots across three blocks; unsuccessful spider-removal and ordinary lighting arms were pooled (60 lit versus30 dark). Removal is not a validated predator-presence contrast. The predation assay counted immobilized Drosophila remaining after two hours and used a binomial GLM, not survival analysis.

Results p16: damaged-leaf frequency was34.6% versus28.6%; this is6 percentage points, not6% relative change. Damaged area was9.5% versus8%, without clear evidence of a difference. Preserve frequency and area as separate endpoints. No sampling variance can be recovered from these rounded summaries alone.

Decision: field experiment retained for extraction, with treatment pooling, repeated plots, and final-version checks pending. No effect added. Do not mix its reported predation ratio with hazards or trap counts. Raw unpooled plot data are the next acquisition target.

Independent primary-source review confirmed all three endpoint/pooling distinctions. Removal changed some non-spider taxa, so preserve original treatment labels upon raw-data recovery. The reported predation ratio is not established as either a hazard ratio or an odds ratio.
