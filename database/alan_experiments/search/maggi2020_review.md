# Maggi2020: light × grazer manipulation

[Primary Methods/Results](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.13485) were read via indexed publisher text (2026-11-20). March–May2016, Mediterranean high shore: four sites, randomly assigned two lit/two dark; one dusk–dawn white LED per lit site, mean27.33lux. Each site contained three grazed and three exclusion quadrats, plus two procedural controls. Quadrats and repeated dates are subsamples; independent lighting n=2/arm, not12.

Final pigments/bacterial samples were collected26May; fluorescence was measured18May. Snail density decreased under lighting. Diversity responses differed between cyanobacteria and other bacteria. Biomass showed a light×grazer interaction, but post-hoc pairwise contrasts were nonsignificant; scytonemin was unaffected. Sequence counts are not organism abundance. Snail density is not grazing rate or predation.

Eligible field manipulation in principle; main-catalogue inclusion remains held. Site-level light×exclusion contrasts retain within-site covariance. Shared infrastructure/units with [Maggi2018](https://doi.org/10.3354/meps12769) remain unresolved; different successional stages do not prove independence.
