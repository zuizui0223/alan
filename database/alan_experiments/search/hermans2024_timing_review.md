# Hermans2024: concurrent insect activity and capture attempts

Reviewed 2026-11-20. [Primary institutional PDF](https://edepot.wur.nl/662180), DOI10.1016/j.scitotenv.2024.173699, Methods2 and Results3 read.

Seven Dutch forest-edge sites had white/red/dark treatments from the Light-on-Nature infrastructure.2020 monitoring yielded19/19/18 complete treatment-nights, not that many independent sites. Camera detections do not identify insect species or unique individuals. Bat feeding buzzes represent capture attempts; buzz/pass ratios are not successful captures or prey mortality.

Table1 reports log-insect slopes from a zero-inflated beta buzz-ratio model with logit link. Preserve printed estimates separately: neither these slopes nor their differences are lighting lnRR. Cross-treatment covariance and model component/output are needed. Red's printed interval excludes zero whereas Results3.2 says only white increased significantly; retain the discrepancy rather than infer a corrected test. The temporal models adjust date; they do not establish causal mediation by insects.

Potential overlap with other Light-on-Nature reports requires site/year/endpoint mapping. Do not count this programme independently merely because the paper is new. No wet/dry comparison or floral link is measured.

Dataverse metadata for [10.34894/Z6PAIA](https://doi.org/10.34894/Z6PAIA) is public, but both CSV files are restricted. No restricted download attempted. Hold quantitative pooling pending data/covariance and overlap adjudication; full-text review complete.
