# Spoelstra2017: bat activity under experimental spectra

Reviewed 2026-11-20. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2017.0075), [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5454258/), Methods 2a–d and Results read through indexed primary text. Direct PMC requests encountered a browser challenge.

## Primary design and findings

Eight Dutch sites each contained four transects: white, green, red and permanently dark. Each transect had three or five posts. Detectors sampled repeated nights in2012–2016; nights after any detector failed were discarded across all transects of that site. Responses were acoustic passes in three bat groups. White/green reduced Myotis/Plecotus activity, increased Pipistrellus activity, and had no detected effect on Nyctalus/Eptesicus. Red responses resembled dark controls. In2014, sticky-sheet insect activity and bat passes were measured together during lit and temporarily unlit nights; usable coverage was seven and six sites, respectively. The article identifies long-term Light on Nature infrastructure. Original data: [Dataverse handle10411/20867](https://hdl.handle.net/10411/20867), also [Dryad10.5061/dryad.1g3r5](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.1g3r5). The Dryad file is `BatsPerTransectPerNight_sonochiro_min5_3gr.txt`. [Supplement collection](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.3780143.v1).

## Extraction implications

Transects receive treatments within eight site blocks; posts, recordings, nights and years do not supply independent illumination replicates. Preserve shared dark transects, bat-group covariance and repeated-night effort. Do not combine routine dark transects with temporary switch-off observations as independent controls. Acoustic passes measure activity, not individual abundance, feeding buzzes, prey attacks or moth mortality. Sticky-sheet area is a capture/activity proxy, not prey consumption. Source treatment models pool years, so a final-year extraction and a pooled-series estimate answer different questions.

Flag shared Light on Nature infrastructure with Cieraad2019 and other Dutch moth/bird reports. Different years do not establish independence; match site/transect IDs before assigning programme covariance. Exact Cieraad overlap is not yet verified. This field design is potentially eligible; raw-data extraction, final-time support and programme adjudication remain pending.
