# McNaughton2021: retrofit comparison requires separate estimand

2026-11-20. DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2020.108944 . Publisher abstract/introduction and a partial indexed Results excerpt read; final Methods/Results not fully reviewed. [Publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006320720310028) and [Griffith repository](https://hdl.handle.net/10072/402124) direct access returned403. Author publication list has no corresponding PDF link.

The study follows26 residential properties through an18-month HPS-to-LED retrofit. This is not automatically an illumination-versus-dark comparison. Indexed Results mention control and retrofit groups; their actual lighting, allocation and before/after covariance must be verified. Do not infer that a group called control is dark or treat every property as an independent lighting allocation.

Spectrum, intensity and light distribution change together. Small retrofit effects therefore cannot be entered as zero ALAN effects. Concurrent animal responses do not measure trophic mediation. Retain in the candidate registry pending full design review; no finite effect added and no title-only exclusion.

Next: recover Methods/supplements, establish comparator exposure and temporal design, separate retrofit effects from ambient-brightness associations, identify shared samples and uncertainty before deciding the separate retrofit evidence table.
