## Design and decision

Methods 2.1–2.4 describe a seven-day indoor experiment with Ampithoe valida feeding on Ulva. Night illumination was 10 lux versus darkness. There were reportedly eight 100-mL containers per sex per treatment, four animals per container; repeated observations and animals are not independent lighting replicates. Lamp-level replication is insufficiently explicit.

Under the existing protocol, retain as laboratory mechanism context, outside the field/outdoor-mesocosm main stratum. Do not expand eligibility retrospectively to obtain an aquatic effect.

## Unresolved reporting

Table 1 reports residual df=24 despite the stated 32 containers and four-cell design. Section 3.1 repeats chi-square=1.146, df=1 for both sexes with opposite significance labels. Preserve these inconsistencies; do not infer losses or correct values. Data availability is on request; no public raw identifier is supplied. No contact was initiated.

No effect or variance was added. Rounded means and ANOVA p-values do not resolve arm-level variance, independent units, or temporal aggregation. Methods/Results reading is complete; final eligibility adjudication remains separate.

Independent review confirmed the scope decision and both discrepancies. Under a standard four-cell model,32 observations imply28 residual degrees of freedom; chi-square1.146 with1df gives p approximately0.2844. Neither computation identifies the correct original data.
