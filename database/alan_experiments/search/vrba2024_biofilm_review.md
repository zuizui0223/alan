# Biofilms: photoperiod systems versus subsamples

2026-11-20. Read Methods and Results of bioRxiv v2,
[10.1101/2023.06.11.544504](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.06.11.544504),
from the public JATS XML. The bioRxiv API explicitly identifies the published
version as [10.1016/j.aquatox.2024.106972](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aquatox.2024.106972).
The final publisher page returned403; its Methods were not read. The
[institutional abstract](https://espace.inrs.ca/id/eprint/15687/) agrees on
the10-day ALAN-by-DDBAC design, but is not a substitute for the final Methods.

## Design verified in v2

Natural biofilms colonized glass slides in a pond before transfer to controlled
room channels. Four treatment systems combine DDBAC absent/present with
14h light/10h dark or24h light. Each system has three parallel channels;
night irradiance is20µmol m−2 s−1, not20lux. No lux conversion is made.
All systems initially received overnight light before the photoperiod split.
This is not an outdoor manipulation despite the outdoor origin of biofilms.

Methods2.3.1 explicitly describes three sampled slides as pseudoreplicates.
Five fluorescence readings per sample, microscopic fields, sampling dates and
lipid samples do not establish independent illumination systems. Four systems
across four factorial cells do not support estimating cell-level between-system
variance. Do not use table/figure n=3 or4 as independent lighting n.

The sampled endpoints include photosynthetic yield, taxonomic cell density,
microfaunal density, pigments and lipids. These are not direct trophic-transfer,
grazing, predation or plant reproduction measurements. Reported strong DDBAC
effects cannot be attributed to light. Unclear light responses must not be coded
as zero. Pigment samples have n=1 and are explicitly limited by the authors.

## Disposition and versions

Exclude the v2 subsample-based variances from quantitative synthesis unless
independent treatment-system replication is documented. Keep it as reviewed
evidence with a quantitative-extraction hold. Indoor mesocosm scope alone is
not used to exclude a community experiment under the current protocol.

SSRN10.2139/ssrn.4485240 has the same ordered ten authors but a different title;
Crossref lacks abstract/version relations. Record probable overlap only. Its
full text is not reviewed or merged automatically. The final DOI lies outside
the original search. Do not count versions as independent experiments.
