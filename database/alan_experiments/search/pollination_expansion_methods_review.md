Reviewed2026-11-21. These resolve scope for two reports, not statistical evidence that ALAN has no effect. The third remains inaccessible. Original abstract-screening decisions are preserved as historical decisions.

## Briolat2021: measured spectra, modelled visual contrasts

Primary https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-24394-0; PMC8260664 XML retrieved through EuropePMC. Methods combines reflectance measurements from70museum moth specimens (14species),140flower samples (14species), foliage/background spectra, and14artificial/10natural illuminants with receptor-noise models. Results compare chromatic contrasts for moth floral detection, wing signals and blue-tit detection; background-choice consequences are simulated. Sample counts and bootstrap replicates are not independently illuminated ecological units.

The study contains informative optical measurements and model predictions, but no measured plant reproductive response, visitation frequency or actual predator attack outcome in a randomized field light/dark experiment. Do not convert Delta-S or its bootstrap interval into a pollination/predation lnRR. The main comparison to equal-intensity moonlight is spectral, not an ecological lit/dark population experiment. Retain as mechanism-model evidence, separate from the field/mesocosm effect pool. No plant- or predator-level outcome was extracted.

## vanLangevelde2011: spectral capture experiment without darkness

Primary https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2011.06.004. Full primary PDF recovered at https://www.csbg.ca/BofD/2011%20vanLangevelde%20-%20Spectra%20Attraction%20of%20Moths.pdf . Methods2.1 describes18portable traps, six fluorescent lamp types, three lamps/type per trapping occasion. Lamps were randomized over18locations on each of12trapping nights during12July–25August2009 in one2.3ha wet meadow at Kampina. Trap positions were at least50m apart. All six treatments are illuminated; there is no dark control.

This is a valid spectrum-comparison field experiment, retained separately under the prespecified spectrum-only category. The wet-meadow location does not make it a wet-versus-dry test. Its112captured species and212trap-moment records are not independent light/dark experiments or evidence of112declining species. Spectral composition predicts which moths are captured, not successful pollination, natural abundance decline, or predation. No dark mean or light/dark variance was inferred.
