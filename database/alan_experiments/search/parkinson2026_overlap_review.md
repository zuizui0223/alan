# Seasonal capture report: overlap hold (2026-11-21)

New supplementary-search lead: [Parkinson, Wagner and Tiegs2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/fwb.70236). Indexed [author-uploaded primary text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405251057_Temporal_Variation_in_the_Impact_of_Artificial_Light_at_Night_on_Aquatic-Terrestrial_Insect_Fluxes), Methods2.1–2.5 and Results3.1–3.3, was inspected; a local PDF was not recovered.

The experiment used Waterfowl Pond, Seven Ponds, 30 fixed stations (five treatments×two placements×three), sampled seven nights during June–September2022 including June3. Existing Parkinson2023 covers June3 at that pond with the same treatment structure. This strongly suggests overlapping samples; exact station/date identity is unverified. Seven dates are not seven independent experiments, and this report must not become a third independent series in the origin-capture meta-analysis.

Pan-trap catches measure capture, not production. Biomass was estimated using lengths/allometry for families comprising90% of catches. Date-dependent colour responses were reported; data availability requires author request. No author contact occurred. Raw repeated-station observations and final-date covariance remain unavailable.

Next: reconcile June3 counts and station IDs before extending the existing series; retain the historical one-night estimate separately. Final adoption and effect extraction remain pending. Original-search coverage and existing pooled estimates are unchanged.
