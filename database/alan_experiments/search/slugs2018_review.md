# Slugs2018: one illuminated field and one control

[Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1002/jez.2170), [repository PDF](https://repository.publisso.de/resource/frl:6416102/data), Methods/Results read2026-11-20. Two managed fields600m apart beside a drainage ditch: one illuminated, one dark. Each had12 installed posts;24 traps per field were monthly subsamples, not independent illumination replicates. HPS operated from2012 until June2015, followed by white LED. Day/night pitfall collections were separate.

Arionidae catches increased more at the illuminated field; the temporal interaction was evident for daytime catches, not nighttime catches. Pitfall capture combines activity and density and does not measure plant consumption. Increased reproduction, reduced predation and insect-carrion subsidy are proposed explanations, not measured mediators.

The abstract's24-streetlight wording differs from Methods'12 per site. Use one field per treatment for replication. This Westhavelland infrastructure is shared with other riparian lighting papers, not an independent programme per publication. Keep descriptive, unreplicated field evidence separate from replicated-effect meta-analysis. Raw trap-level numbers cannot resolve the missing treatment-site replication; no main effect row added.
