# Merckx2023 diapause scope and replication review

2026-11-20; indexed AGR:IND608023533 title matches primary report DOI10.1111/1365-2664.14373. Detailed AGR API metadata timed out, so retain the original record key pending identity adjudication.

[Primary Methods/Results](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2664.14373), sections2–3, and [institutional PDF](https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/bitstream/10024/44355/1/nbnfi-fe2023060852877.pdf): indoor split-brood study with one ALAN room and one control room. Families and source populations do not replicate lighting independently. North/Mid-European groups also differed in photoperiod and experimental timing; their difference is not a common-photoperiod latitude experiment.

Endpoints are diapause versus direct development, larval duration and pupal mass, not overwinter survival, predation or pollination. The ten-month overwintering describes prior-generation maintenance. Preserve reported developmental evidence outside field-interaction pooling; do not derive lighting precision from hundreds of larvae. Separation in the binomial model also makes a large interaction coefficient unsuitable as a finite generic effect.

Earlier Merckx2021 supplied critical-photoperiod information, not demonstrated duplicate measurements. Independent agent confirmed design and endpoint limits. This is not second-human extraction.
