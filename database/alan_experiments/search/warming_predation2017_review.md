# Warming × lighting × predator experiment

[Primary Methods/Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5647293/) read on 2026-11-20. Wisconsin alfalfa field, 2014:48 cages in four randomized blocks, crossing light, warming and three predator treatments. Each cage began with20 aphids; final abundance was measured after9 days. Four cages per treatment combination are the independent units, not individual aphids. Light treatment was approximately1500 nW/cm²; no spectral conversion to lux is inferred.

The response depended on predator identity and warming. Separate indoor1-hour feeding trials measured consumption, but field final counts combine reproduction and losses; they are not observed predation rates. Predator recovery was incomplete. Treatment covers create a highly controlled dark comparator.

Results text reports a three-way F=15.71, p<0.001, while Table1 gives F=4.3, p=0.021. Retain the discrepancy; neither is an extractable signed lnRR with sampling covariance. A light×predator contrast, stratified by warming, is the relevant field estimand.
