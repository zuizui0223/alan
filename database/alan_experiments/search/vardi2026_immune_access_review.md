# Vardi-Naim2026: outdoor survival lead, full-text hold

Reviewed 2026-11-20. [Publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0269749126001442), DOI10.1016/j.envpol.2026.127774; [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41651394/). Abstract describes outdoor enclosures with Acomys dimidiatus and A.russatus, immune/endocrine rhythms, antibody responses and survival. This is potentially relevant outdoor survival evidence, not an indoor-only exclusion.

The abstract's2.35-fold mortality-risk statement lacks an extracted model scale, uncertainty, independent enclosure allocation and cause-specific deaths. Do not treat it as a predation effect, infection transmission effect or weighted lnRR. Immunity does not establish the pathway causing mortality. The publisher reports data available on request; no author contact made. Institutional metadata and ResearchGate did not supply full Methods/Results in this pass. Keep full-text/design review pending and no finite effect added.
