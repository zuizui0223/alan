Eight maternity-roost sites had repeated hedgerow observations. Two lamps/site are not independent replicates. Night order was fixed: control, generator-only, four illuminated nights, generator-only. Highest-activity hedges were selected initially. Retain order and selection limitations.

Endpoints were acoustic passes and first activity after sunset, not individuals or prey capture. Reported onset means were29.9minutes control and78.6minutes lit; their descriptive difference is48.7minutes. Paired variance and exact aggregation mapping remain unresolved; no derived confidence interval was assigned.

Activity figures report back-transformed means/SEM, not arithmetic count means/SD. Opposite hedge observations covered five sites and received light spill, so they are neither independent sites nor true dark controls. No group-mean lnRR added. Retain this as illumination avoidance/timing evidence, separate from prey mortality and plant pollination. Obtain site-night data before quantitative integration.
