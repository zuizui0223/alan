# Bhardwaj 2020 independent scope review

Source: accepted manuscript, DOI [10.1016/j.jenvman.2020.110313](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2020.110313); cached `../gbif_annual/alan_experiment_sources/bhardwaj2020.txt`. References below are printed manuscript pages and numbered manuscript lines. Methods and Results reviewed; final typeset version not separately checked.

Four 16-night trials comprised four baseline, eight lighting, and four recovery nights, with simultaneous dark bridge/culvert controls (pp.6–8, lines119–154). Eleven physical sites (five bridges, six culverts) supplied sixteen site-trial deployments: eight lit and eight unlit. Five sites were reused; three changed from dark to lit and two remained dark. These are not sixteen independent sites (Table1, p.9). Site random effects explicitly address reuse (p.14, lines263–269).

Eight detectors per structure were averaged within above/under positions to estimate nightly acoustic passes. Individuals were not identified; passes indicate activity, not population size (pp.10–13, lines183–234). No prey consumption, attack success, or feeding-buzz endpoint was measured. Results show overall activity decreasing underneath and increasing above lit structures; species responses varied (pp.15–21, lines285–350). Road mortality and trophic benefits are interpretations, not measured outcomes.

Decision: exclude from predation-effect pooling; retain field movement/activity evidence. Any separate activity synthesis must retain physical-site reuse and repeated-night dependence. Bayesian credible intervals are not automatically sampling SEs. The indicator example at line258 appears inconsistent with the preceding during/after definitions; verify code before coefficient extraction.
