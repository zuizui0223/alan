# Frugivory2014: field fruit removal and conditional timing

[Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.12206) read via Chapter2 of the [author dissertation](https://refubium.fu-berlin.de/bitstream/handle/fub188/52/Dissertation_Lewanzik_D.pdf?sequence=1), indexed full text; local download returned a challenge, not the thesis.

Costa Rica:14 Piper sancti-felices plants experienced both dark and sodium-light conditions, initial order randomized, with at least three dark nights after illumination. Field illuminance averaged57lux. Fruits and nights are repeated observations within plants; shared foragers may reduce independence further. Separate captive choice trials must not enter as independent field observations.

Field removal was63/63 fruits in darkness and49/63 under light. These pooled counts do not provide plant-cluster variance; the authors'89.5% model prediction is not the observed77.8%. Conditional on harvest,12 paired observations had reported dark/light means84/196 minutes after sunset, SD42/82, paired t=-4.1. Rounded summaries permit only an approximate conditional mean-difference interval, kept outside the lnRR catalogue.

Removal does not measure seed deposition, germination or recruitment. Delayed harvest among harvested fruits cannot represent time-to-removal of all fruits. Main quantitative removal extraction awaits plant-level records; no variance is inferred by treating126 fruits as independent units.
