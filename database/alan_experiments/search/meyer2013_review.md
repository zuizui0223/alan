# Meyer2013: stream–riparian exchanges

[Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1890/12-2007.1), [author-hosted PDF](https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/u.osu.edu/dist/1/24733/files/2020/03/12-2007.1.pdf), Methods/Results read2026-11-20.

Nine reaches were surveyed across ambient illumination in2010–2011. The manipulation increased lighting at five previously low/moderate reaches to10–12lux in2011. All five received light; no contemporaneous unmanipulated reach was included. The contrast is August2010 versus August2011, not a simultaneous lit/dark trial. Monitoring and weather checks do not supply a missing control. Three traps per reach and repeated collections are subsamples.

Reported responses included reduced tetragnathid web density, aquatic-emergent family richness and mean body size, and increased terrestrial-arthropod mean body size. Web counts proxy spiders, not attack rates. Net flux and terrestrial biomass responses were not conventionally significant. Reflection is proposed, not experimentally isolated. Printed paired-test degrees of freedom conflict with five reaches and reported probabilities; do not infer precision from those summaries.

Keep ambient-gradient and uncontrolled before–after components separate from the main simultaneous-control effect catalogue. AppendixD contains summary statistics, but reach-level paired covariance was not recovered. No lnRR was extracted from abstract percentages. The source supports a contrary response direction, not proof of a light-specific decline or wet-versus-dry effect.
