## Design and scope

Kellettville Firefly Farm, Pennsylvania, 41.5521, -79.2526, June 16–July 5 2019. Four physical forest-edge plots (2.5 by 10 m, spaced 30 m), each divided into four subplots. Blue, broad-spectrum amber, red and unlit control lights were hung in a predetermined random order each evening. Sixteen survey nights are repeated observations, not sixteen independent parallel plots per arm. Subplots and repeated observer surveys also are not independent illumination replicates. Between-plot attraction interference cannot be excluded.

Male Photinus carolinus flash patterns were counted in 60-second surveys. Individuals were not tracked: fewer patterns can reflect fewer males, less flashing per male, or both. This is not mating success or insect population decline, and is not an interspecific interaction endpoint.

Sticky traps were deployed on 15 nights, moved with light assignments, and replaced every 1–6 days. Yellow-trap observations were discarded after switching to clear traps nine nights into the experiment. Analysis used trap area multiplied by deployment days as an effort offset. Small numbers of spiders and phoretic mites were omitted. Total survey nights do not determine the number of retained clear-trap samples. No predation, flower visitation, pollen transfer, fruit set or seed production was measured.

## Numerical record, not adopted meta effects

`reported_means.csv` transcribes eight author-reported means and SE from Results pp.6–7. All light treatments had lower flash activity and higher sticky capture than dark controls according to the author's reported comparisons. Amber produced the lowest flash activity; blue and amber the highest capture. Only four fireflies were trapped across all illuminated treatments. Capture and flashing concern different biological measurements and must not be pooled as a single population response.

Do not derive independent-group variances by assuming n=4 or n=16. The reported SE aggregation level, retained trap sample count, repeated plot/date dependence and covariance with the shared dark control are unresolved. SE itself does not always require n to calculate a contrast variance; the unresolved design and covariance are the reasons for the hold here. Raw data and code are available on request according to both reports; none have been recovered and no author contact was made.

The preprint prints an irradiance of 1.37 x 10^20 photons cm^-2 s^-1 at 60.8 cm. This requires source/unit verification; no silent correction or lux conversion is made. It must not enter a dose moderator in this form.

## Decision

Field experiment with a dark control: do not exclude as spectrum-only. Retain as capture/behaviour evidence pending final-version and independent-unit data recovery. No rows added to the main effect catalogue; no interspecific mechanism or wet-versus-dry effect inferred. An independent reading of preprint pp.5–7 confirmed these design and variance holds.
