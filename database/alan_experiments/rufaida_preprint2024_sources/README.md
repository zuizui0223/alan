## What was actually manipulated and measured

- Species: *Lepidostoma hirtum* larvae, collected March 2022 at Zielona Struga stream, 52.997208 N, 18.450469 E. These are **collection coordinates**, not the experimental room coordinates, and must not be used to extract experimental wetness, climate or baseline ALAN.
- Setting: **darkened laboratory room**, after one week of aquarium acclimation. Experimental arenas were 10 cm dishes with aeration, three shelter stones and water held at 15°C.
- Lighting: 2 lux at the water surface under warm LED (2700 K) or HPS (2000 K), versus darkness <0.01 lux. Daylight was simulated by incandescent lighting from 07:00 to 19:00.
- Cue manipulation: homogenate of two crushed conspecifics in 10 mL distilled water was filtered and added to cue-treatment dishes. No live predator attacked the study animals. The text does not clearly specify an equivalent added-water sham for no-cue dishes.
- Design as reported: four larvae per dish, 15 minutes acclimation, 45 minutes recording, 3 light × 2 cue conditions, 16 trials per cell (96 total). The text does not establish unique-animal reuse, independent lamp replication, or all trial-order/randomization details. Neither 384 animals nor 96 trials is adopted as an independent lighting n.
- Responses: movement-time fraction, distance travelled, shelter-time fraction, mean and minimum inter-animal distance. These are short-term behaviours, not actual predation, survival, emergence, litter consumption, plant growth or reproductive output.

## Results and numerical limits

The preprint reports reduced movement time under LED/HPS without cues. Shelter use depended on both light and cues, with greater refuge use under LED with cues; distance travelled and grouping measures had no reported light/cue effects. The displayed table captions are inconsistent with the narrative numbering of response panels (the narrative refers to Table 1 A–E, whereas its caption lists four responses). The recovered XML contains figure/table references and captions but no numeric table cells. We therefore retain qualitative directions and do not invent mean/SD/n or infer variances from significance statements.

This version belongs to the **indoor behaviour-only evidence layer**, outside the prespecified outdoor/mesocosm/greenhouse ecological-response synthesis. “Aquatic” does not imply “outdoor” or provide a wet/dry comparison. No effect is added to the 108-row catalogue or the 114-row reported-statistics registry. The finding is not a direct test that ALAN increases predator pressure on aquatic insects.
