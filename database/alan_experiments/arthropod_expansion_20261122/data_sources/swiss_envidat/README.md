# Swiss forest streetlights, capture checks

The 2024 Environmental Pollution article (10.1016/j.envpol.2024.124209) and the Conservation Biology article (10.1111/cobi.70137, 2025 online, 2026 issue) use overlapping samples from the same 3-forest WSL experiment. Data are archived under 10.16904/envidat.496 and 10.16904/envidat.566, respectively, both CC0. The original downloads are in the numbered directories, with landing metadata from the public CKAN API. See source_manifest.json for stable download URLs and file hashes.

Rebuild the summaries with Python 3 and summarize.py. These are descriptive checks, with no catalogue modification, synthesis-ready variance or pooled estimate across studies. The target exposure period remains all published summers, 2021–2023 for flight interception and 2021–2022 for ground pitfall activity-density. Each of the three forests contains 12 lit and 2 dark plots. Three nominal temperatures, two intensities and two shapes distinguish lit plots. Any-light summaries collapse these configurations, which share controls. Repeated samples, years and endpoints from a lamp are dependent. Flight specimens were caught at lamp height during the night. Ground pitfall traps operated both day and night at the centre position. Taxon sums in the archived endpoint files match SumAll on every retained row. Adult flying insects include groups with aquatic juvenile stages; extracting only strictly terrestrial life histories requires a more restrictive compatible taxon grouping.

Following the source script regression_pttrf_allgroups.r, retain nominal temperature 2000/3000/4000 or control, intensity 50/100 or control, and issues equal to zero. No flagged rows were imputed. Within each plot and nominal year, take the arithmetic mean of available weekly SumAll observations. Arm means weight plots equally. Arm SDs are sample SDs among plot means. The two-year ground result weights each of the two year-specific means equally per plot. Missing trap weeks make this a different estimand from a full 4-week complete-data mean. The original study fitted mixed models of log(count+1) and marginal contrasts. These summaries do not recover those model coefficients. The source R code uses week_ID alone for one flight model; the paper describes weeks nested within years. Preserve the raw data for reanalysis. No between-arm variance is provided because the site blocking, finite replication per design combination and missingness need to be carried into a prespecified model.

arm_summaries.csv gives all-site and separate site results. plot_summaries.csv retains the identifiers and observed weeks. flagged_flight_records.csv marks three issues with the nominal flight keys: L12 contains different treatment labels at week 3 in 2022, L15 appears once in the retained lit intensity range, and 28 otherwise eligible 2023 rows contain an Excel date in 2024. In 2022, the retained flight rows give 37 lit and 7 dark identifiers, with overlap in L12. The design requires 36 and 6. No 2022 or full-period flight arm estimate was adopted. The 2023 check uses the archived nominal year, flagged. All 2021 and ground checks are provisional, including the whole-period ground description.

Ground capture overlap is directly supported by 306/307 eligible center-trap records in .566 with the same site, plot, collection date and trap position and seven identical individual taxon counts in .496. An Alpthal A01 date lacks an exact match. The two archives group taxa differently. Study-specific SumAll totals are generally different and reporting both as independent evidence would duplicate samples.

The .566 Moran file provides coordinates for some plots. The numeric ranges resemble Swiss projected coordinates. native_coordinate_values.csv preserves the values under the source field names and does not convert coordinates or assume this represents dark plots. No exact control coordinate was inferred.
