# Melbourne dark and experimental LED plots

Robert et al. (2025), 10.1002/ecs2.70291, gives the ecological results for a four-reserve experiment. Version 1 of the authors' Figshare dataset is 10.26181/24311368.v1 (CC BY 4.0). Attribute the underlying data to Kylie A. Robert, Alicia M. Dimovski, Peter Contos, Nyil Khwaja and Stephen R. Griffiths. source.xlsx is the complete unchanged 62,795-byte file retrieved from https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/42689083 on 2026-11-22 JST. The authors' MD5 is in metadata.json. The workbook Information sheet explicitly links its insects to the earlier methods paper. The matching sampling dates, 4 reserves and 16 traps support treating 10.3791/63156 and 10.1002/ecs2.70291 as one experimental family. The public workbook and full ecology paper supply the overlap evidence.

Run summarize.py with Python and openpyxl. Insects_Order contains 96 observations and a grand total row with no date or treatment. The former includes four reserves × four permanent experimental plots × two phases × three nights. All order counts sum to the reported daily abundance, and their sum agrees with the footer of 375 (not 750). The baseline was 27–29 March 2021 and the authors' during-night labels were 30 March–1 April 2021, with the last trap collection the following morning. The sample row labels and spelling are preserved. Nightly flight-intercept capture measures activity/attraction. Three distinct spectra (A amber, FW filtered warm white, CW cool white) share the same dark control C in each reserve. The paper names and maps four reserves; the caption's Melbourne coordinates are metropolitan, not plot-level exposure coordinates.

The capture checks use site-blocked difference in changes on the arithmetic captures per night scale: average the three nightly counts within each plot and phase, subtract that plot's baseline mean from its during mean and subtract the simultaneous same-reserve control change. The estimated mean and its descriptive SE come from the four reserve contrasts. Site-by-arm baseline and during means are in site_capture_summaries.csv, arm-specific contrast means in paired_capture_checks.csv and covariance of the three spectral effect estimates in paired_capture_estimate_covariance.csv. The all-lit average is an alternate three-spectrum description of the same sites, so do not count this as a fourth independent contrast. Several site × arm × phase means are zero; no arbitrary replacement, uncorrected log ratio, or published-model replication is asserted. These descriptive reanalyses have not been adopted into the common synthesis.
