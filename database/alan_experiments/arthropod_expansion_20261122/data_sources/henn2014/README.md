# Henn et al. (2014): paired drift-net counts

Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/02705060.2014.900654 . Coordinates are from Methods, and counts from Table 1 (p. 312), visually checked against the authors’ PDF. The five Texas streams are paired experimental blocks; each has one illuminated and one ambient two-hour net sample on different nights. Sampling order was randomized within streams. Approximately 1,482 lx versus ambient <2 lx.

`site_pairs.csv` retains the original five site pairs. Lit counts sum to 1,229 and ambient counts to 1,961 (3,190 total). The Results narrative reports 1,212 and 1,979 (3,191 total). This discrepancy is unresolved and no number was repaired. Net counts are not the volume-standardized drift density used for the authors’ nonsignificant paired comparison. No effect estimate or sampling variance from these counts has been adopted into the synthesis. These are five stream blocks within one programme, not five independent studies or ten independent nights. Coordinate precision is as reported; exact trap-position accuracy is unknown.
