# Arthropod abundance and activity: search expansion, 22 November 2026

This dated amendment implements the requested focus on experimental arthropod abundance/activity. It is recorded before screening this search snapshot. It is not a preregistration: previous ALAN studies and results are already known. Base repository commit: `db82976f834cc048e76e69e6f621ba11e3ef33e5`.

## Search and record handling

Run a supplementary Europe PMC title/abstract search through 2026-11-22 without a mandatory experiment/manipulation term. This addresses one identifiable recall limitation of the original 521-record search. Do not restrict to wetland, positive results, open access, or a language. The search remains limited by Europe PMC's indexing and the declared terms. It does not complete or replace Web of Science or Scopus. Follow original studies cited by reviews as a separately logged supplementary source.

Preserve raw responses outside the public repository, page URLs, hashes, query, timestamps, hit counts, and all bibliographic records. Match to the existing 553-report inventory by exact verified DOI, index identifier, or an existing PMCID crosswalk. Flag title similarity for review only. A new index record or report is not a new experiment. Keep preprint/final-version and shared-experiment relationships explicit.

## Rapid screening and minimum extraction

Prioritize field/outdoor mesocosm studies manipulating ALAN against an actual dark or lower-light control. Preserve observational, indoor, all-lit spectrum-only, and unreplicated designs in separate queues. Do not change their prior exclusion/hold decisions merely to increase k. Screening uses titles/abstracts before full Methods review; missing abstracts or data do not mean exclusion.

Record source identity and location for every value. The minimal extraction comprises treatment/control means, SD/SE and its definition, independent allocation units and sample sizes, site coordinates with precision, exposure duration and spectrum/intensity, observation effort/window, shared controls/paired design, and programme identity. Mean, dispersion, sample size and coordinates alone do not resolve pseudoreplication or mismatched sampling effort. Detailed audits can follow after this triage, but the allocation unit and endpoint must be resolved before pooling.

Use separate outcome strata for (1) passive interception/pan/pitfall captures or activity-density, (2) census abundance/standing density, and (3) aquatic emergence or drift. Keep flower visitation, prey capture/predation, and plant damage/reproduction as secondary stages. Active light-trap catches under ambient lighting require a separate detection-method stratum. A single pooled abundance/activity coefficient is not assumed. Do not reverse signs to manufacture a common harmful-effect scale.

For repeated samples, summarize at the actual experimental unit with matched effort, preserving blocks, dates, paired covariance and shared controls. Multiple doses, taxa, years and publications are dependent observations. Do not fill unknown variances, add pseudocounts to zero means, or infer SE from a p value without a valid model scale and degrees of freedom.

## Wetness moderator and analysis gate

Prioritize reported plot-to-water distance, then coordinates with known precision and an appropriate historical water layer. These characterize landscape water proximity, not plant moisture affinity or measured soil moisture. Keep aquatic origin and water proximity separate. A near/far contrast within a programme is stronger for this question than a between-study contrast confounded with country, trap, spectrum or taxon. Do not infer that grassland or no mapped open water means dry habitat.

Build the endpoint-by-programme-by-water-context support table before fitting a moderator. There is no automatic adequacy threshold at 20 publications/effect rows: independent programmes, within- and between-study wetness range, leverage, dependence and uncertainty determine feasibility. No expanded pooled estimate or mechanistic mediation of the observational plant result is claimed by this search batch.
