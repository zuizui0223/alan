## Design and eligibility

Methods lines 86–231: indoor incubators at 15 C, simulated solar/lunar cycles, six ALAN levels 0, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 10, 50 lux. Zero ALAN retains simulated moonlight, so is not an absolute darkness condition. Adults originated from South Milton Sands (barnacles, March 2022) and a Lyme Bay mussel farm (April 2022); larval exposure was in Plymouth laboratory incubators. Source collection coordinates must not be substituted for experimental environmental exposure coordinates.

Barnacle Austrominius modestus: three parents, four vials per parent per lighting treatment, 72 vials with initially ten larvae each. Experiment ended at day32; size was available for 63 vial averages. Mussel Mytilus edulis: one sperm donor and two mothers, two rearing densities, four vials per mother-density-light combination, 96 vials. Day9 length used ten larvae per vial; day60 size/growth used 77 vial averages owing to mortality. Vials, sampled larvae, parentage and observation days do not establish the number of independent lighting installations. Incubator replication and illumination allocation remain unresolved.

Exclude from the main field/mesocosm synthesis under its current scope, retain as indoor larval-performance context. The two taxa are not two field experiments. Rearing density was manipulated within each species; no interspecific competition, predation, pollination, facilitation or ecosystem-engineering function was measured. Habitat-forming species identity alone is not evidence of a measured community effect.

## What the Results support

Results lines 232–290 report declining survival with increasing irradiance. The numerical control/high-dose comparisons are model predictions: barnacles 67 versus 58 survivors per100; mussels approximately46 versus20 per10,000. Their intervals are labelled prediction intervals, not raw treatment mean confidence intervals. Do not enter these as means with inferred SD and n into effects.csv. The mussel statement about falling below50% refers to the relative predicted survival compared with control, not absolute50% survival. No pooled effect or dose slope is extracted.

## Unresolved source inconsistencies

The accepted manuscript labels irradiance as W m2, alongside lux values; verify units before any dose use. Growth equation (D60-D9)/D9 is dimensionless but labelled micrometres. Methods describe day60 size/growth from77 vial averages, while text/Table2 retain residual df around954–958. The exact handling of zero irradiance in the described log transformation is not stated. These are manuscript-level unresolved issues, not corrections we can infer. No raw-data reproduction or final-version error claim is made.

An independent reader confirmed the indoor scope, vial counts, predicted-versus-observed distinction and the above inconsistencies. Main effect catalogue and existing three exploratory syntheses remain unchanged.
