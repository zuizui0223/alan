## Joint-support geometry comparison
In Li, require simultaneous C/P/L support for the same species × round within a field. Preserve zero outcomes. Compute arm means within strata, then equal weight strata within fields, then equal weight fields. For both lighting arms, average log contrasts, not arithmetic average of ratios. The downstream log contrast D=log(RR_seed)-log(RR_fruit); its sampling variance includes the paired field-score covariance. Field n=3, t2 descriptive intervals. For strict all-fields support only SN (one species, one round) remains; SN endpoint labels use original id, other species original Label. These do not establish independent repeated individuals. All observed is a nonmatched comparison menu; common exact two and common distinct two differ because duplicate labels persist in one stratum. No favorable support chosen after calculation. Starting-bud-normalized ratio of overall means is a separate diagnostic, not the same as average per-field opportunity-normalized outcome.

## Two-programme diagnostic
Boom published post-fruit RR=(estimated seeds/flower RR)/(fruit-set RR); white .688, green .507, red .791, equal log menu .651. If the reported marginal SEs are commensurate, the sharp algebraic covariance bounds for the linear delta score with absolute marginal SD terms a_i are [max(0, 2 max(a_i)-sum(a_i)), sum(a_i)]. The all-color SE bound is [0,.825] on log scale; unknown covariances are not recovered. Shared dark enters once with coefficient -1. Treating n of flowers/ovules as lighting replicates is prohibited. Equal retention q in infested fruits gives reconstructed post-fruit point RR .643 at q=0, .751 at q=.5, .826 at q=1; arbitrary independent qL/qD in [0,1] yields .463–1.147. This assumes the conditional uninfested seed/ovule proxy can be used for potential output; q is not observed. The point direction is not identified without that assumption.

Conditional REML+mKH k=2 df1 diagnostics, 6 Li supports × 3 illumination menus × 5 Boom spectral menus × 3 outcome definitions (published, q0, q1). Sweep 201 grid points between the hypothetical marginal variance bounds (zero variance configurations with zero denominator omitted). **270 scenarios, 54238 calculated grid fits**; 236 scenarios have all grid intervals spanning 1, 34 contain at least one interval excluding 1 under unverified variance assumptions; at the maximal variance endpoint 270/270 include 1. These counts are sensitivity calculations, not independent studies/tests. For the all-color published/equal-log comparison, all six support menus have all their grid intervals including 1.
