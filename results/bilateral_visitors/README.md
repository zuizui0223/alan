# Bilateral timed flower visits in the frozen light comparison

The primary island-minus-mainland light slope difference remains −1.9017206221 percentage points per log1p(VIIRS), with 10,698 site-years in 78 cells and 195 cell-years. The matched geographic frame uses its original 5° cells and the five calendar years 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025. A focal plant must match one of the 544 WCVP names and have a plant record in that cell-year. A survey observation can occur anywhere within the cell; the GSHHG survey-side assignment uses the report's coordinates. Observations are not assumed to be co-located with the frozen plant photos.

## Expanded search

* [Budburst public reports](https://budburst.org/data) yielded 815, 583, 1,830, 1,363 and 923 pollinator reports respectively, using the public search defaults excluding youth observations and including unofficial plants. Event detail pages provided elapsed minutes and counts for different visitor groups. Of 176 matched taxon/place/year reports, 156 had positive time and an interpretable broad insect count, including observed zeros. Those reports represent 55 species and eight cells. Broad counts use the Bees & other bin on the [three-group form](https://budburst.org/resources/3group-pollinator-sheet_english), or identifiable bee/wasp/fly/beetle groups on the [detailed form](https://budburst.org/storage/statamic/7grouppollinatordatasheet_english.pdf). Both omit mixed butterflies/moths, birds, bats and unidentified visitors. The broad bin is less taxonomically resolved. In the two bilateral cell-years all records reported zero in the pooled butterfly/moth category.
* [UGA public survey layer](https://services2.arcgis.com/PYn6bWCjT6bhw1z3/arcgis/rest/services/survey123_956c41fc2fa34638bb3342ac06905588_results/FeatureServer/0) had 480 mapped records before screening. One mainland record for Rubus trivialis matched in 2025 (15 minutes). Several taxon entries were blank. No opposite-side timed record in that cell-year was found. The [regional census results](https://gsepc.org/census-data/) aggregate a much larger program; the campus layer does not represent the whole census.
* [Wanshan Island](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.t76hdr8bj) provides 15-minute 2019 counts in frozen region 58_22. The other site of that study was another island surveyed in 2018. A [Hong Kong thesis](https://hub.hku.hk/handle/10722/280296) reported Bidens alba and Lantana camara, both in the local 2019 plant cohort, in a nine-site survey. The available abstract does not give counts and minutes by park, side and plant. The thesis full text could not be retrieved in this run. This potential pair remains unresolved.
* The [Australian Wild Pollinator Count](https://www.inaturalist.org/projects/wild-pollinator-count) public photo project accepts records outside formal timed counts. [Brazil FIT Count](https://doi.org/10.3897/biss.7.111545) documents a compatible timed protocol. Public region-year event counts and plants on both sides were not established; the international FIT Count regional-results page requires authorized access. The [previous five-source audit](../shared_visitors_regional/README.md) found no bilateral cell-year for its matched positive records. These search results are a lower bound on survey coverage.

## Florida

The only verified cell was 19_23 (85–80°W, 25–30°N). In 2023 the island report was from Longboat Key in May, and all seven mainland reports were on October 5, across two nearby coordinates. Seventy mainland minutes are summed observer-minutes; overlapping observers are possible. In 2024 each side contributed one report. The two island plant species and the two mainland-year plant lists are available in bilateral_results.csv. No species was surveyed on both sides in the same year. The seven-group coding alone supports both sides only in 2023.

For each year separately, the original plant-share response and the 544-species response were regressed on island × log light, the six frozen covariates, and the original land-class balancing weights. Both designs had full rank; island and mainland radiance ranges overlap. The island slope uses just two plant site-years per year. Timing, host selection, flower abundance, survey protocol and frequency can change together. Neither a between-region moderator nor a general association with pollination compensation can be estimated from one cell. No inference or confidence interval is attached to these within-cell descriptive contrasts.

The regional frame lists all 195 cell-years and counts eligible Budburst reports separately by side. The US candidate event table gives identifiers for the public records; missing counts are unverified. For a replay, acquire survey pages with scripts/acquisition/alan_us_timed_visits.py and run scripts/analysis/alan_bilateral_visitors.py with the survey cache (including the frozen s.parquet and shared.parquet tables), v0.11 inputs and output directory. The parent analysis constructs the frozen site table and the site-by-shared-species table.
