# NEON repeat monitoring of open flowers

Source: [NEON plant phenology observations, DP1.10055.001](https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP1.10055.001). NEON records open flowers as present, absent or uncertain on visits to tagged plants and also records intensity. Observation records link to plant identification and location by individualID. [NEON user guide](https://data.neonscience.org/api/v0/documents/NEON_phenology_userGuide_vE?inline=true)

The public site catalog lists 47 sites for the product. The NEON location hierarchy supplies 102 named phenology plot locations. We also retrieved their 194 child locations in or near any retained five-degree block. We intersected point coordinates and years of product availability with the frozen region-year and cell-year frame and separately applied the study's high-resolution GSHHG land assignment to the location coordinates. A plot marker documents a potential sampling location. A site-level year of product availability does not establish that a species, or an open-flower status, was sampled at that marker that year. Public plot markers do not locate every individual. [NEON product catalog](https://data.neonscience.org/api/v0/products/DP1.10055.001) · [Example NEON plot location](https://data.neonscience.org/api/v0/locations/SERC_063.phenology.phe)

Thirteen NEON sites in six regions have plot or marker locations in frozen region-years. All 910 matching marker-by-year positions classify as mainland in GSHHG. In the exact 0.05-degree cells, matching markers belong to DSNY in 2019 and 2025, OSBS in 2021 and SERC in 2025. They all have the frozen mainland-dark classification. Puerto Rico's two NEON phenology sites and Hawai‘i's Pu‘u Maka‘ala site fall outside the original five-degree regions.

NEON's full monthly download requires a token. The anonymous endpoint returned HTTP 403. The producer's open teaching archive supplies original monthly files for BLAN, SCBI and SERC in the target year 2019. We used the individual tables for locations, preferred individual-specific coordinates when present, and otherwise screened plot-level coordinates. We retained the latest edit of a known open-flower status for each individual-date after deduplication. Species binomials and available author strings were matched to the frozen WCVP taxonomy, requiring an unambiguous accepted ID. The archive contained 13,981 usable open-flower status records in 2019. All three archive sites lie in region 20_25, which is absent from the frozen 2019 region-year frame. Consequently the archived individual records contribute no arms, including when the plot-level fallback is allowed. Remaining monthly files were not assessed for individual-level flowering states. [Open teaching archive](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/22775042) · [NEON tutorial and access terms](https://www.neonscience.org/resources/learning-hub/tutorials/neondatastackr)

The three CSVs report plot support, archive status by frozen region and archive status by frozen cell arm. The last two are header-only because no archive observations matched. Run scripts/analysis/alan_neon_phenology.py with the private NEON download cache, the prior photo-frame cache, and the v011 directory containing wcvp.zip and coast. The script verifies the teaching archive, WCVP and coastal layer digests. The plot metadata were retrieved 2026-11-12.
