# Arthropod and reptile predator source checks

Screened direct studies of adult moths with orb-web and bolas spiders, ambush predators, hornets and geckos on 2026-11-12. The frozen frame was read as the 195 distinct (region5, year) pairs of universe.parquet derived from the original 10,698 cells. Region indices are floor((longitude+180)/5) and floor((latitude+90)/5). Geographic matches require the actual sampling year. The Japanese flower-ambush dataset is treated separately in the parent audit.

| Study | Observed outcome and denominator | Intersection or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Yuen & Bonebrake, Hong Kong | Six sites including Lantau, Lamma and mainland localities. Experimental light/no lamp and paint controls. In 163 camera-hours investigators counted 5,375 approaching moths, 300 web contacts and ten consumed moths. Re-summing the 21 setups in the public workbook gives 4 consumption events per 84.5 lit web-hours (0.047/h) and 6 per 78.5 unlit web-hours (0.076/h), pooling all sites and paint treatments. Web contacts were 116 and 184, respectively. The reported negative lamp coefficient was for contact with a web. | Region 58_22 appears in all five target years, but the experiment occurred in August–November 2015 and 2016. The published contrast concerns local experimental lighting. [Methods, Fig. 1 and results](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.4070) · [Raw spreadsheet](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.4070/supp-1) |
| Diaz & Long, Kentucky | Three trees near Lexington, Sept 11–17, 2021. Five filmed captures by four bolas spiders. Recording targeted active spiders after bolas formation. The 10–15 minute tree censuses counted hunting spiders and bolases. | All three coordinates printed in Methods fall in 19_25. This region is absent from all 195 pairs. A separate catch filmed in 2022 also lies outside the year window. [Methods and results](https://doi.org/10.3390/insects13121166) |
| Yip et al., Xitou, Taiwan | The deposited LED-experiment.csv has 49 web treatments, ~196.63 video-hours, calendar years 2018 and 2019, and four separate counts of firefly/other attraction and interception. The residual category pools nonfirefly taxa. | Xitou is in 60_22, absent from the frozen region-years. The table cannot identify the moth-specific fraction of the residual category. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.70102) · [Data and dictionary](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.27101089.v1) |
| Lo et al., Daken, Taiwan | Infrared video recorded visits to flowers with or without a crab-spider dummy and recorded prey responses. The experiment gives visits, with moths among nocturnal visitors. | Published coordinates place the two-subsite park in 60_22. Spider dummies cannot produce captures. [Primary study and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00265-023-03291-0) |
| Do et al., South Korea | Video at a willow nest quantified *Vespa binghami* entries and exits in ten-minute intervals. Prey identification used larval meconium, with 46 lepidopteran OTUs out of 74 arthropod OTUs in Supplementary Table S1. OTUs do not give the life stage, time, or count of individual moths captured. | The reported nest at 36°19′28″N, 128°43′28″E is in 61_25, retained only in 2025. Nest-monitoring effort cannot be attached to diet sequence counts to calculate attacks per hour. [Methods, Figs. 1–2 and Table S1](https://doi.org/10.1093/aesa/saae024) |
| Geckos in Pacific and Indian Ocean islands | The Oahu enclosure study counted attempts and successful captures of insects, and sampled termite alates and moths independently by sticky traps after removing geckos. In Mo'orea, diurnal focal follows scored attacks on prey in October–November 2018. Observations across six *Phelsuma* species on islands document nocturnal activity around lights but the published evidence inspected did not establish an in-frame series of moth captures and matching observation effort. | The first two studies predate the target years. The six-species paper supplies evidence of a possible mechanism. [Oahu experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC21710/) · [Mo'orea field study](https://doi.org/10.1670/21-030) · [Six island gecko species](https://doi.org/10.1111/aec.13012) |

There were no eligible four-arm region-years among the above sources. A standardized European camera-trap dataset, for example, follows adult moth detections over 196 nights but its individual record table counts moths detected by a camera or captured by a survey funnel. Using trap catches as a predator numerator would change the outcome. [German field data](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.m37pvmdgz)
