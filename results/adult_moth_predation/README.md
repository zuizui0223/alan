# Adult moth attack and capture rates in the frozen ALAN frame

The historical island-minus-mainland coefficient of −1.902 and the secondary 64-species pollen-limitation result retain their previous status. We sought a separate response, the number of predator attacks or successful captures of adult moths, with the number of moth encounters/examined individuals or the time over which the outcome was scored. Captures per attack, captures per moth encountered, and captures per observer-hour have different denominators. An attack or encounter may involve the same moth more than once.

## Geographic support

We screened all 78 original 5° regions in their 195 retained region-years during 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025. Sampling sites could be anywhere in the frozen region-year. The light classes use a common regional threshold at the median of the original site-year radiances for the calendar year. Confirmed direct wild adult outcome series with matching effort contributed zero four-arm regions. The material recovered did not permit estimating an island-minus-mainland difference in the predation-rate response to night light. The zeros in the coverage table count eligible series or regions; they are not observations of zero attacks.

## What the original tables measure

- In Hong Kong's region 58_22, six sites spanned islands and mainland. A field lamp experiment recorded 5,375 moth approaches, 300 web contacts and ten consumed moths. The raw records gave four consumed moths in 84.5 web-hours with lamps and six in 78.5 hours without lamps. Sampling occurred in 2015–2016. The two pooled rates describe the lamp treatments; they do not estimate a land-class interaction or a regional VIIRS slope. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.4070) · [Raw data](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.4070/supp-1)
- Western Honshu contributed 12 lepidopteran records in the 2019 predation-events worksheet, but the named localities occur in 62_24 or 63_24, both outside the 78 regions. The separately timed mantis experiment was conducted in 2020. Its night moth subset records 39 encounters, 16 attacks and five captures. These counts include moths approaching the flowers and moths landing. The predation proportion across moth encounters is 5/39 and the success proportion per attack is 5/16. The study's overall nocturnal visitor statistic includes other insects. [Raw workbook](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.13596197) · [Methods](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.3482)
- One 2025 study reported eight cases of feeding on male pine processionary moths already held by sticky pheromone traps in two retained mainland regions. The Portuguese forest group recorded five ant-fed moths against 366 automated catches at five traps. All five known events occurred in two traps but the trap IDs are not published. This is a proportion of all forest catches with reported feeding. The Portuguese bright urban group had 114 catches and no explicit predation count. Three moths were reported eaten by grasshoppers in the Italian forest group, but the publication does not establish which traps supply the matching denominator. The relative light assignment uses the published group centroids. Predator attack time of day and individual-trap radiance were not recovered. [Manuscript, awaiting peer review](https://ore.eu/article/view/1616) · [Public workbook](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22735263) · [VIIRS and effort](trap_feeding.csv)
- Other searches covered birds, bats, geckos, spiders, wasps, mantises and other predators, including direct field trials, cameras, trap studies and predator diets. Camera detections, disappearance from a trap, feeding buzzes, larval attacks, models and diet occurrence alone did not supply the requested outcome. For example, a Chilean video shows a wasp attacking a live adult moth in flight but its repository record describes a selected event without total surveillance or the number of flying moths at risk. The Spanish mammal study identified adult female moth consumption through fecal material. The global experiment at 21 sites deployed paper moths with mealworms. [Chilean video](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28701569) · [Spanish study](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecs2.70542) · [Global experiment](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.8931zcs0j)
