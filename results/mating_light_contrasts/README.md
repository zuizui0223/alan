# Floral reproductive traits and the island–mainland light contrast

The primary −1.902 estimate retains all 10,698 frozen site-years. This secondary analysis represents each species by its contribution to community composition (100 times its recorded presence divided by the number of photographed plant species at that site-year). No photo record contributes zero observed share. This is not a true absence or population abundance estimate.

Both hypotheses target the signed island-minus-mainland slope; a more negative value means a larger difference in the direction of the primary result. Species receive equal weight. For GloPL, the predictor is each species' median published log reproductive response ratio across its available hand-pollination experiments. The fitted linear trend across 64 species is not weighted by number of experiments. For breeding system, categorical means are shown for every class, with a direct SI minus SC comparison. Partial compatibility, self-sterility and a conflicting entry retain their own rows.

Coefficients are calculated in the frozen balanced region-year design. Because all species have the same design and denominator, the regression of species-specific slope differences on a trait is identical to fitting the appropriately weighted species composition contrast to the site-years. Species-mean contrasts and predicted quartiles use that same identity. Reported uncertainty uses the original 78 region clusters conditional on this species set and external trait assignments. It does not propagate trait measurement error, phylogenetic dependence or uncertainty in extrapolation to other species. The p column and confidence limits are nominal. The two focal comparisons yield Holm-adjusted p values of 0.088 (pollen limitation) and 0.983 (SI minus SC). Quartile predictions transform the same continuous fit and are not additional independent tests.

The support table describes where any qualifying plant was photographed, independently of how many frozen sites enter the model. The experiments measuring pollen limitation took place outside the primary five years and did not isolate moth pollination. Photographic prevalence and shared habitat associations can affect the species-level composition slopes.
