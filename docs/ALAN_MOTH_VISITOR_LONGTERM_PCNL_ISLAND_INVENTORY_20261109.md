# ALAN | Moth-visitor-positive island plant records × historical nightlight (1992–2024)

**Status (2026-11-09): runnable source-only ingestion / aggregation / PCNL exposure code, not a new fitted association.** The verified 93,209,351 original-photo registry contains 34,346,428 photo IDs assigned to 1,836 WCVP accepted plant species with a GloBI moth-family flower-interaction record. These are **documented moth-positive plants, not demonstrated obligate moth-pollinated plants**.

## Biological question

Do islands/site-years that have been exposed to higher *historical* ALAN contain a smaller recorded share of plants **independently demonstrated to rely on nocturnal moth pollination**, and does that relation differ with alternative daytime pollinators, self-compatibility, lifespan and geographic isolation?

This program builds the **source-positive numerator support only**. It DOES NOT yet estimate that answer. Unrecorded GloBI moth visits cannot be used as a negative or daylight-only control; 34.35m moth-positive photos divided by 93.21m photos is **not** the fraction of moth-dependent wild plants.

## Phase A — island/group metadata census

On each of the original 16 **observation-hash-disjoint** source groups, put its 14 `moth-positive-original-tier-XX-group-YY.csv.gz` files in `rosters/` and its original `original_moth_photoID_groupYY_verified_receipt.json` alongside the source. Example for group 0:

```bash
python -m scripts.analysis.alan_moth_species_historical_pcnl_inventory_20261109 inventory \
  --rosters rosters --group 0 \
  --extract-receipt original_moth_photoID_group00_verified_receipt.json \
  --wcvp src/wcvp/mois350399_wcvp_source_taxon_exact_name_resolution.csv.gz \
  --accepted src/wcvp/mois350399_wcvp_accepted_native_distribution_TDWG3.csv.gz \
  --coast src/coast --db moth-island-group-00.sqlite
```

- Uses source **date, coordinates, WCVP accepted name**, GSHHG exact original-island polygon inclusion and a 0.05-degree cell.
- One photo ID counts as one *photo opportunity*. An observation UUID counts once even if it has many photos. A plant taxon is counted once per island / year regardless of observation count.
- Keeps true source 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025 photo years, not missing years interpreted as plant disappearance.
- GloBI fields retained separately: generic moth visit, database `pollinates`, Walton direct nocturnal pollen-carriage, butterfly co-positive. None means effective local moth dependence.
- Original photo IDs with ambiguous overlapping polygon membership, off-island locations or unverifiable taxonomy are excluded *and audited*, not changed to a biological zero.
- Group receipts verify **all 14 original compressed file SHA256 hashes** and photo-ID totals against the prior independent source extraction receipt. Rejects reclassification or source silent mutations.

After all 16 group databases are present, run:

```bash
python -m scripts.analysis.alan_moth_species_historical_pcnl_inventory_20261109 collect \
  --db-dir group-dbs --out-dir result-islands
```

Outputs include `moth_island_species_year.csv`, `moth_island_year.csv`, `moth_island_all_years.csv`, `moth_island_site_year.csv`. The collector requires all **34,346,428** original published moth-positive photo IDs to be accounted for across the source input receipts **before** claiming exhaustiveness. These tables are observational records, not a census of native plant abundance.

## Phase B — 1992–2024 time-harmonized nightlight

Use **one consistent, cross-calibrated raster family**: Li & Cao's [PCNL dataset v3, 1992–2024](https://zenodo.org/records/17013414) (the Zenodo record includes `PCNL1992.tif` through `PCNL2024.tif`; ~2.9 GB in total). This dataset corrects/intercalibrates DMSP-OLS and VIIRS. Do **not** concatenate raw six-bit DMSP digital numbers and VIIRS nW/cm²/sr or compare the resulting beta to ALAN's unharmonized contemporary VIIRS beta.

Place the 33 downloaded georeferenced TIFFs in `pcnl/` and run:

```bash
python -m scripts.analysis.alan_moth_species_historical_pcnl_inventory_20261109 exposure \
  --site-year result-islands/moth_island_site_year.csv \
  --pcnl-dir pcnl --output result-islands/moth_site_year_pcnl.csv
```

For each photographed site×year, calculate `sum(log1p(PCNL annual value))` for **1992 through year−1**, plus its average and valid-year count. A missing annual pixel means unknown cumulative exposure, **never zero**. A 2024 photo uses 1992–2023 only; a 2025 photo uses 1992–2024.

Site-level results and `moth_island_year_with_longterm_ntl.csv` are exported. **The latter sums photo/site species richness across sites and is NOT unique island species richness.** Use `moth_island_year.csv` / `moth_island_all_years.csv` for distinct island species. The exposure summary uses equal site weights, not preferentially photographing a common species. PCNL satellite radiance remains a proxy for surface light, not illuminance experienced by a flower or moth.

The PCNL source is an independent product, not yet restored into this branch. Until all original group artifacts and the 33 original PCNL rasters are acquired and validated, **no numerical island-specific counts, exposure values or regression coefficients are claimed here.**

## Mandatory gates before ecological hypothesis tests

1. Create a **full-93m original photographic reference frame** by WCVP taxon × same island × cell × year with true sampling effort. Do not calculate a moth-positive species *proportion* with only the moth-positive numerator or interpret GloBI-nonrecord as moth absence.
2. Independently grade plant species as nocturnal **visitor / pollen transport / verified effective pollination / experimentally quantified dependence**. GloBI `pollinates` and Walton moth pollen carriage do not prove exclusive dependence. Obtain actual comparison plants with verified diurnal service.
3. Require external evidence on self-incompatibility, lifespan, native/introduced status and local substitute visitor presence/effective service. Island's existing exact-island five-channel inventory has **0 adequate Lepidoptera nondetections among 8,265 source-available islands**; it cannot by itself label islands as moth-loss/moth-retained. Lepidoptera includes butterflies; it is not a moth-specific local-service signal.
4. Report same-year site support, historical-light overlap, matched region/urban intensity, land use, photo/observer effort, spatial clustering, family/genus composition, geographic block holdouts, and historical exposure cohort **before** an ALAN slope. Similar predicted signs alone cannot rule out urban floristics/horticulture and observer bias.
5. Cross-sectional correlation of cumulative ALAN with photo-recorded species does not establish species decline, local extinction, floral evolution or causality. A temporal occupancy outcome needs repeated, comparable survey opportunity.
