# Four-arm diel pollination exclusion and functional nonadditivity (2026-11-24)

This is a secondary, exploratory reanalysis of the author-extracted arm values in Kendall & Nicholson (2025, Ecology Letters, DOI 10.1111/ele.70036), not a new systematic review of primary experiments and not a pooled ALAN effect. Author CSV retrieved from GitHub blob 39a82d966afcdde670603add1c881ace8791ffe9, commit 1945b7c114ad87000608ce6018d0280001c04ef1, data/DvN_LiteratureDataClean_2024-09-13.csv. Data DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.26004538.v1. Raw file: 1652 rows, 145 plant labels and 140 study labels; not the published final meta-analytic sample (135 studies,139 plants).

Four treatment arms matched by study, site, coordinates, year/month, plant, cultivar, extra treatment and endpoint; 184 complete unique groups before restrictions. Require finite nonnegative means, n>1, identical units, open mean >0. Fruit outcome limited to proportion-labelled units and all arm means in [0,1]. Seed limited to recorded per-flower/per-ovule units or appropriate seed-set proportions. Do not average absolute seed counts across units. No sample-size weighting, variance imputation, phylogeny or independent primary-source design confirmation. Full cells and source row IDs are retained. Primary extracted fruit proportion subset: 58 cells, 39 plant×study, 31 study labels, 38 plant labels.

Let O,D,N,B be open, day only, night only and complete insect exclusion. Loss of night L=(O-D)/O; night-only net contribution A=(N-B)/O; relative nonadditivity I=L-A=(O-D-N+B)/O. B may include wind or other non-insect reproduction; it is not necessarily autonomous selfing. No truncation of negative losses or ratios >1. Saturation of bounded fruit proportions alone can yield negative I; this is functional outcome nonadditivity, not necessarily ecological compensatory behaviour. Thresholds of 0.2 (night-only contribution) and 0.1 (night exclusion loss) are exploratory descriptive choices.

Aggregate cells within plant×study, then plant×study within study, equal weight across studies. 9999 resamples of study-label means, percentile intervals, seed 7125. These intervals quantify variation across selected source study labels; they do not include within-study sampling variance or phylogenetic/primary-programme dependence. Not a formal inverse-variance meta-analysis.
