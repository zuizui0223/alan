A positive or negative *direction* is useful in an evidence map but is **not** a meta effect size. The numerical ratios above are **not exchangeable** across seed/initial-bud, selected capsule, developed seed/ovule, formed-fruit-dependent seeds, and conditional seed-estimate formulas. Where an author reported significance without recoverable estimate or cluster SE, do not infer numerical zero or a Wald variance from a p-value.

## New externally verified positive grey-literature lead — not tenth paper

**Lofchick, Koren, Sapir & Gruntman (2023)**, *Artificial light at night and its consequences for the pollination of wild plants*, Entomological Society of Israel 41st conference, **printed p. 58 / PDF index p.61** ([original abstract](https://entomology.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Israel-Entomological-Society-2023-abstracts-1.pdf)). It describes a common-garden ALAN experiment and preliminary **Silene palaestina** results: **lower daytime pollination but higher nectar and seed production** under ALAN. A [Tel Aviv University botanical-garden project page](https://lifesci.tau.ac.il/botanical/research/Silenepalaestina01) also identifies the plant, ALAN and seed-production research. This is a potentially *additional positive direction*, but **NOT** a verified independent peer-reviewed publication, not an estimable numeric treatment effect, and not a valid new meta-analysis k. The name may also appear as *Silene palestina*; check original vouchers and any subsequent papers/theses before deduplicating.

**New source-specific retrieval target:** ascertain whether 2023 Silene results were published or deposited as an MSc thesis or subsequent article, obtain original lighting-assignment replication, light/dark flower→mature seed means, uncertainties, phenology and pollination access regimes. If unavailable, keep as *excluded from quantitative synthesis / awaiting author data*. The conference report's “positive effect” does **not** establish p<0.05 or causation from any particular nocturnal insect.

## Decision / priority

1. **P3 stage-specific evidence synthesis is feasible as a systematic evidence map now**, with a multilevel meta-analysis only for a stage whose original independently assigned experimental units, common scale, and variance have been recovered for >=3 studies. PR #77's 1,304 summary-contrast source must not be treated as 1,304 new plant ALAN studies.
2. **Main theory-driven A β_V remains unestimated (k=0 eligible)** until original dark-day/open V and open ALAN fecundity effects are co-located and jointly clustered in sufficient truly independent populations. Highest-return original cell recovery: Lu 2026 and Macgregor 2019; they alone provide only two source studies.
3. **Positive-effect discovery matters:** retain Macgregor, Li and the unverified Silene candidate, and keep Boom's uncertain net output, rather than selecting negative endpoints. Morphological moth syndrome or global GloBI moth-visit labels are NOT a surrogate for same-population functional V.
4. **Island × ALAN reproductive interaction remains unestimated**, despite source-verified island/photo and diel-exclosure descriptive associations. Prior observational positive findings are hypothesis support and a separate spatial paper, not additional experimental replication.

The files and checks are *source-adjudicated post hoc*, not PRISMA-complete or prospectively preregistered. No original author data were redistributed, no authors contacted, and the existing 12-reproductive-paper denominator was not altered.
