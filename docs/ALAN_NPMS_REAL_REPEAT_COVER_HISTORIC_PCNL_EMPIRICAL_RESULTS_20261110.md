## Scientific judgment / decision

**No reproducible negative ALAN association appears for positively documented NPMS vegetation cover** in 2015–2025 on these source-verified repeated plots. One plot-equal positive coefficient has a nominal 95% CI crossing zero; source-cell-equal and source plant-panel-equal slopes are almost zero, stronger within-plot light-variation gates show small uncertain positives, and survey-specific slopes are mixed. The external more independently studied GloPL plant source subset also gives an uncertain negative. Thus no manuscript should claim either light-caused plant decline or a protective ALAN effect on vegetation from these data.

Interpretation limitations remain material:
- **Positive-only/selection:** Missing original NPMS species entries were never turned into real local absence or cover=0; conditioning on positive records cannot assess local extirpation, initial colonization, total community disappearance, or changes in the probability of record. A repeatedly observed plant is a survivor-selected group.
- **Actual source light resolution:** Original corrected PCNL is ~1 km and the annual 0.02 loglight changes can be grid corrections, not flower-level lux or a local new streetlamp. Using 0.1/0.3 gates helps but does not prove a natural experiment.
- **NPMS original field protocols:** 87/154/155 are distinct methods, source DOMIN scale can be harmonized but not all target species are on every survey list; original observer switching, habitat management, season, weather, mowing, grazer pressure, regional land cover and detection heterogeneity are not fully controlled by plot+plant/year fixed effects.
- **GBIF/GloBI labeling absent here:** This NPMS test never measured real moth or butterfly visitors, a gecko predator, nocturnal pollen transfer, seed set or plant reproductive success.
- **Global relevance:** UK NPMS is a regional repeated-plot system and cannot estimate a world-island vs continental interaction. The small Channel Islands subset cannot substitute for globally replicated independent island contrasts.

The source-supported next step is NOT further p-value searching. It is to establish an independent spatially-and-temporally matched biological *reproductive or nocturnal visitor* outcome on the same experimental sites, or to explicitly report the negative detection/sampling limits of the present observational global island-photography data.
