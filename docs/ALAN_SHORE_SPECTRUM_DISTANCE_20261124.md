## SevenPonds2022 placement

Source: Parkinson et al. 2023 DOI 10.1098/rstb.2022.0361. 30 unique trap stations, 3 per colour x placement, 4699 organisms (source figure origin classification, includes spiders). One pond, one night, about 15 m separation of littoral and riparian placements; not a measured individual flight path or a continuous distance gradient. Violet 410 nm intensity not standardized against the other colours; no inference about pure wavelength at common intensity. Use fixed extracted source units and taxon rows, verify identical origin totals at every station. Lit mean is arithmetic average of colour-cell means with equal weights, divided by same-habitat dark mean. Ratio of littoral to riparian light-response ratios. Covariance between outcomes retained in same-cell scores, delta variance, Welch t interval; 3 stations per cell and shared dark cells, possible attraction interference, all intervals nominal. Per-colour values reproduce prior table; new menu sensitivity and taxonomic exclusions exploratory. No pseudocount for zero arm. Caenidae and Leptoceridae source labels 2860 and 686 counts. 2120/2454 (86.4%) of aquatic catch in littoral violet cells are these two taxa. Removing both leaves all-colour placement contrast 2.885 [1.012,8.226]; removing violet as well gives 2.337 [0.768,7.117]. Triple contrast, aquatic vs terrestrial response ratio between habitats: 1.915 [0.218,16.810]; excludes Ephydridae 2.220 [0.253,19.441]. Thus not demonstrated that spatial amplification is aquatic-specific, or that ordinary streetlight spectra have same amplification.

## Distance-scaled macro sensitivity

Fixed b780370 coordinate_trends.csv.gz and main distance.csv joined by unit_id (1706 valid). Distances are to 1999 GLAD water pixel centres (>=50% detection), capped 1 km, not measured flower/shore distance or lamp distance. Valid <=20/40/60/80/100/200/500 m sites = 49/81/96/124/148/260/565; brightened =14/22/29/37/42/79/155. Near-distance cutoff choices 40,60,100 m, inspired by literature and previous 100m tabulation, exploratory not fitted thresholds. Fit original 100 km block fixed-effect model with same controls incl. hydrology, replace continuous near by cutoff indicator and its light interaction. Sensitivity add continuous near and light interaction. Exclude blocks without light variation as original. All sample 1674 sites/53 blocks; brightened only 316/33. Existing continuous slope reproduced (3.895332 and 1.888481). Cluster score variance follows original finite-sample scaling. In-session JS t quantile used Cornish-Fisher approximation (prior continuous CI matches to <1e-5); saved Python replay uses scipy exact t, negligible differences. Full-sample 40m positive coefficient +5.88 (CI +1.12,+10.65), contrary to hypothesized stronger near-shore decline; sensitive to continuous-distance adjustment (+3.59, CI crosses zero). Brightened estimates all span zero. Most information concentrated in limited spatial blocks (e.g., effective blocks 2.89 for 60m brightened). No multiplicity adjustment, no threshold selection, no causal identification. Thin water channels, intermittent wetlands, pixel scale and plant coordinate precision constrain this bridge. Response is candidate-minus-reference recording-rate slope, not abundance or reproductive rate; cohort selected partly from same years.

## Independent literature context and acquisition

Carannante et al. 2021 DOI 10.1002/2688-8319.12053: Study A lit/dark pairs at 3 and 40 m; Study B light-only 3/10/20/40/60/80 m, six transects and six nights. Approx 80% reduction in caddisfly captures 3 to 10 m, mayfly sharp drop between 20 and 40 m; Diptera (size-filtered, not all aquatic-confirmed) more gradual decline and irregularity at 10m. Lit-only distance pattern conflates baseline abundance gradient with response to lamps; not a measured attraction radius or causal distance x light interaction. BORIS item still inaccessible, raw data not extracted.

Schofield et al. 2023 DOI 10.1111/fwb.14177: two rivers in NZ for colour experiment; distance experiment at one Hawdon River over six nights, 1–100 m, randomized distances, 4 colour temperatures but paired sets on different nights. Author reports 58%,65%,81% of caddisfly catches within 10,20,50m, respectively (Hydroptilidae excluded from total caddisfly analysis); cumulative 50% distance about 9m (3000K),10m (4000K),20m (5650K),30m (6500K), temporal pairing confounding acknowledged. Captures at 100m remained positive. No raw file available, author request required per data availability. Avoid pooling cumulative catch proportions or converting them to actual attraction distances. Same thesis is not another independent programme. No additional quantitative ALAN effect inserted.

## Scope

Within-study spatial heterogeneity is plausible and independently reported in other regions, but empirical bridge to wet-plant macro result not supported. Next: actual waterbody-specific exposure, lamp distances and water/land connectivity; ideally light x distance paired lit/dark at every distance and matched insect/plant endpoints.
