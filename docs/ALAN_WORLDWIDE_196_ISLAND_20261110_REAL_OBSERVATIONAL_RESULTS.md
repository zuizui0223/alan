## Exploratory main result: photographed moth-family record ratio

Response: percentage of GBIF HUMAN_OBSERVATION records of Noctuidae+Geometridae among records of those two moth families and Nymphalidae+Pieridae at the actual same original island/photo site/year. It is not individual moth density or effective floral pollination. Model has year+island fixed INTERCEPT per true island × observed year; predictor log1p(PCNL at site center in preceding calendar year), controls original total four-family photographic records as log1p(event count) and site-center latitude, and weights EACH island equally. Nominal confidence interval clusters by 196 independently identified GSHHG islands.

- Main 196 islands / 62,688 observed photo source cell-years: slope **−0.8651 percentage points per unit log1p prior-year PCNL**, nominal island-cluster 95% CI **[−1.9737,+0.2435]**, p=.1254. NOT a resolved primary negative light effect.
- Independent source count control: log1p(total four-family records) responds to prior satellite light by β=+0.0168, CI [−0.0265,+0.0601], p=.446. However the recorded moth source fraction covaries strongly with recorded event count within the source model: +6.72 pp per log total event, CI [4.98,8.46]; an observer/source reporting issue, NOT automatically causal visibility bias or true moth behavior.
- Island log10 land-area × prior light interaction β=−0.3052, CI [−1.2321,+0.6217], p=.517, not supported. [Original main model original-data execution](https://github.com/zuizui0223/alan/actions/runs/42787302661) SUCCESS.

## Ecological interpretation and next environmental test

These are actual original GBIF records of insects, unlike the older ALAN GloBI moth-visited botanical photo classification, but they are NOT standardized night visits, true insect counts, local population trends or flower pollen delivery. Differences among data pipelines, natural history observers, active evening/night times, platform/dataset, exact observation sites within 0.05° cells and cultivated urban species remain major confounders. GBIF source records do not establish moth biological absence when none are reported, especially with coarse photo effort.

The next explicitly separate source product calculates real 2012–24 annual prior-year 5km PCNL dark land fraction and four-neighbor connected dark patches for the fixed original 62,688 source photo cell-years, using GSHHG coastline and lake exclusion. Conditional test needs mean site-year light and dark-area controls, island-year FE, and independent variation in patch shape. The original PCNL raster is coarse ~1km, and many source neighborhoods may have no independent fragmentation contrast. A failed positivity/rank gate is an honest NOT ESTIMABLE result rather than a null ecological effect.

Until independently collected same-site insects/actual floral contact/seed set or transparent observer-detection controls are available, a supported large-scale conclusion is only about documented GBIF moth-vs-butterfly record composition and its sensitivity to island sampling. Do not claim a causal ALAN moth loss/benefit or a globally proven floral phenotype change.
