Primary evidence ledger: compatible pollen (not simply conspecific pollen) is the proximate link to reproduction. Waites & Ågren 2004 examined 14 populations, with compatible fraction of same-species pollen between 0.02 and 0.27; year-specific population mean fraction 0.12 and 0.18. Correlations of compatible pollen with seeds per flower 0.80 and 0.88; pollen limitation negatively related to compatible pollen. Total conspecific pollen not associated significantly with population size. Incompatible pollen did not show the hypothesized adverse effect after accounting for compatible pollen. No light manipulation. Reference wet species Nymphoides peltata, Hottonia palustris, Menyanthes trifoliata also exhibit compatible-mate constraints. This is a counterexample to treating heterostyly as a candidate-specific trait. Primary studies are examples, not a representative prevalence denominator or comparable effect-size pool. Retain all positive and contrary observations, avoid double counting studies already present in Razanajatovo or GloPL.
