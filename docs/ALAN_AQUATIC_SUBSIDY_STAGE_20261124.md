## Interpretation

The source flow can be represented as emergence E × local movement/availability R × encounter and retention Q, but the cited observations do not measure a common flux and cannot be multiplied or inverted to infer transport probabilities. Candidate hypotheses include localized aggregation, spatial displacement, and consumer dietary reorganization, as distinct from reduced emergence. No plant-side mediation or wet/dry moderation established. Preserve counterexamples (positive/uncertain emergence, negative or unchanged consumer response). Main and existing synthesis unchanged.
