## Core interpretation and next macro data layer

The categorical same-year 39-island negative point coefficient (prior PR #47) survives one-at-a-time island and species omissions, but failed continuous reproductive share sensitivity. Now, in the fuller 514-island repeated CELL source cohort, the island-equal temporal estimate has a broad CI crossing zero and p≈.91. Source documented selfing known/unknown status itself varies with ALAN, and a source-only night-study-documentation×selfing composition interaction depends on weighting/trait quality.

**No robust empirical global evidence** from this photographic flora/proxy matrix supports light-induced effective moth-pollination failure compensated by selfing, or a universal island-specific decline of moth-like plants. This does not demonstrate absence of light effects on pollinators.

Next identifiable outcome must be local effective pollen deposition, pollen supplementation and seed/fruit output under day/night exclusions and experimentally/temporally measured lighting and island native/wild establishment; avoid fitting the same observational source many ways in search of significance. Separate landscape dark-patch connectivity and gecko predation/benefit with true co-located independently measured functional evidence rather than GloBI record counts.
