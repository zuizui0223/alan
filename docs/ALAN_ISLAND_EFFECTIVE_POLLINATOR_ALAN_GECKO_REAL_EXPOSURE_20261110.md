## The next *real* macro-analysis, without changing the claim prematurely

The user goal is to build an island-wide **exposure-at-risk** atlas, not to force a significant moth population or plant decline slope. The present original strict effective-service seed register is too sparse and confounds region with pollination guild. Therefore expand a reproducible trait catalogue in **separate, truth-labeled tiers**:

- **Tier 1 – independently effective pollination:** witnessed deposition, seed set / pollinator exclosure, legitimate pollen movement; full study, accepted species/synonym, archipelago and native/endemic evidence, wild-site status, optional autonomous reproduction. Keep distinct guilds `effective_nocturnal_moth`, `effective_diurnal_bird`, `effective_native_gecko_or_lizard`. A bird visitor or pollination syndrome is not Tier 1 without effective service evidence.
- **Tier 2 – independently documented visits/pollen carriage:** GloBI/field visitation/pollen on animal body; do not use it to claim obligate pollinator reliance, effective service or an evolutionary transition.
- **Tier 3 – morphological/diel syndromes:** flower-opening time, colour, night scent, morphology; hypotheses only.
- **Separate evolutionary-transition axis:** ancestor/sister lineage's independently shown pollinator guild and direction of change. Until then label `ancestral_state_unknown`, even when effective night-moth pollination is established in the derived insular taxon.
- **Separate island-specific invasion / predator axis:** gecko taxa native versus introduced/status with credible source, not genus photo records. Exclude native day gecko mutualists from the introduced predatory-gecko risk score. Preserve specimen scarcity, survey efforts and flowering period.

After this trait expansion and a native island flora independently verified by exact island, measure: `ALAN exposure intensity & trend (before species date)` + `distance / continuity of dark land` + `opportunity of verified introduced nocturnal insect predators`. Restrict same-island native plant reference to **verified uncultivated wild native plant occurrences**, match recorder/platform/season, correct unequal geographic recording effort. The existing WCVP native-at-TDWG3 and GloBI visitedBy metadata alone do not satisfy those requirements.

**Scientific decision:** The original large 93m plant-photo infrastructure supports an empirical native-archipelago exposure *opportunity census*, but the presently strict functional pollinator subset does NOT establish a globally replicated island pollinator-shift vs ALAN/alien-gecko risk result. The key problem is source evidence coverage, not a lack of statistical significance. Do not rebrand 0 Mauritian species photos as ecological absence or infer apparent exposure differences across archipelagos as a selection pressure.
